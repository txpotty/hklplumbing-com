    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Aiea

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck-mount fillers sit on the rim. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Aiea, HI

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Aiea, HI

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. We stock common Delta cartridges. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Aiea, HI

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Aiea

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. We find galvanized supplies that rust shut, valves soldered to copper with no stops anywhere, and spouts threaded onto steel nipples that snap when you try to turn them. We bring transition fittings. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Aiea

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.
