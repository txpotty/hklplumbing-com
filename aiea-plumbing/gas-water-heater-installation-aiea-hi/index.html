    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Aiea, HI

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. A 120-volt outlet is required. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Aiea

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Aiea, HI

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Aiea

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Aiea

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them. Their Defender Safety System uses a sealed combustion chamber and a flame arrestor, so we follow their manual on clearances and the combustion air screen at the bottom of the tank.
