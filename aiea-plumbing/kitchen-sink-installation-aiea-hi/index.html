    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Aiea, HI

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.
