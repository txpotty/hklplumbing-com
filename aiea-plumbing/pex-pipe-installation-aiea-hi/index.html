    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Aiea

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Aiea

    The main from the meter to the house feeds every fixture you own. When an old galvanized or polybutylene service starts leaking underground, the yard stays soggy, the water bill climbs, and the pressure drops at every tap. New PEX goes in its own trench. At the house we add a new full-port ball valve for the main shutoff.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Aiea, HI

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines.     Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Aiea

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think.     PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Aiea, HI

    With PEX-A, the tech slips a ring over the pipe end and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. Cold weather slows it. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Aiea

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. No crimp tool to calibrate. The fittings have a wide bore, so flow loss through each joint stays low.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Aiea, HI

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. Every one is listed for potable water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.
