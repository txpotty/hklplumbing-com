    Softener Loop Installation in Aiea, HI

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Aiea

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. A sound tank can keep its resin and get a new valve. Sometimes the whole unit goes.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Aiea, HI

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Aiea

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. Depending on how old it is, we swap media or the full unit. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Aiea, HI

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. It depends on the valve. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.
