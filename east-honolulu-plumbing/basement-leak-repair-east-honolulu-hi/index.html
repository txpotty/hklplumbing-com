    New Sump Pump Installation in East Honolulu

    Installing a new sump means breaking out a section of slab, setting a lined pit, and running the discharge out through the rim joist. Most of our installs get a cast iron pump, a check valve and 1-1/2 inch discharge. Discharge cannot go into the sewer in most places.     Groundwater Pressure Relief in East Honolulu

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. You may see water coming up at the floor cracks or around the floor drain during a long wet week. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in East Honolulu, HI

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Plan on several days. Our crew handles the footing drain and its outlet, and a waterproofing partner applies the membrane.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in East Honolulu

    Water lines along a basement ceiling tend to leak at fittings, valves and pinholes. Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is usually soft to moderately hard, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. We cut out the bad section and replace it with copper or PEX, and if we find more than two or three pinholes on one run, we recommend repiping that run instead of patching it again. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Crack Injection for Basements in East Honolulu

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy bonds the two sides. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Interior Drainage Systems in East Honolulu

    Where digging outside is not practical, we handle the water from inside instead. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Water then runs behind it into the channel.

    Outside Wall Waterproofing in East Honolulu, HI

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in East Honolulu

    Basement plumbing leak repairs in East Honolulu typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Exposed pipe on an unfinished ceiling is cheap. Opening finished walls or the slab adds cost, and a multi-floor cast iron stack can go past that range.

    Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. Expect two to four hours with drywall access. Seepage repairs vary more, from half a day for an injected crack to most of a week for a full interior perimeter drain, which includes cutting and removing the slab edge, setting pipe and stone, and pouring new concrete.

    Musty odors and damp carpet are the early ones. Peeling paint and white mineral deposits follow. A water bill that climbs or a sump that runs more than usual are worth a call too.

    Plumbing leaks show up no matter the weather. Groundwater leaks track the rain. Turn off every fixture and watch the water meter, and if it still moves, the leak is in the pressurized lines, while water that only appears a few hours after a storm is almost always coming from outside.

    It depends on the scope. Small pipe repairs usually skip the permit. Bigger work like a new stack or a backwater valve may need one, and we pull it.

    Most supply and drain repairs are finished in a few hours. A new sump takes most of a day. Drain stack replacements and slab leak repairs usually take one to two days, since they involve opening finished surfaces, letting any new concrete patch cure, and running a final test before we close things up.

    Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Polyurethane handles wet cracks. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.

    Simple pipe repairs and crack injection usually do not need a permit. Bigger jobs often do. Replacing a drain stack, installing a new sump with a discharge line, adding a backwater valve, or excavating near the foundation may require a permit, and we pull it when it is needed.
