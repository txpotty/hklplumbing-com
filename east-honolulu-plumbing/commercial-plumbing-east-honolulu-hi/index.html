    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in East Honolulu, HI

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in East Honolulu, HI

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. The first step is mapping the risers and branches. Work gets phased by floor or suite so tenants keep their water most of the day.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in East Honolulu

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. Our testers handle RPZ, double check and PVB devices with annually calibrated gauges and submit results the way your provider wants them. If a device fails, we usually rebuild it the same day.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in East Honolulu, HI

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. Our work covers high-input tanks, multi-unit tankless racks and boilers feeding indirect storage tanks. Around East Honolulu, sediment and fine volcanic grit can still build up in aerators, valves, and water heater tanks, so periodic flushing is worthwhile. Mineral scale chokes output quickly. We put the heaters on a descaling schedule and install mixing valves so hand-wash sinks get tempered water at a safe temperature.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in East Honolulu, HI

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless units get a descaling flush. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.

    Tenant Improvement and Build-Out Plumbing in East Honolulu, HI

    New tenants usually need restrooms moved, a break room added or a kitchen built in a shell space. We work from stamped plans, coordinate rough-in with your general contractor and schedule inspections so the plumbing is never what holds up the opening date. Old lines get capped. Abandoned drains and supplies are cut back to the main and capped properly, which prevents dead legs and sewer gas later.

    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in East Honolulu, HI

    Grease does not stay in the trap if the trap is full or the baffles are broken. It ends up in your line. We inspect interceptors, replace failed baffles and outlet tees and jet the kitchen lines that feed them, which removes hardened grease that a cable just slides past. Regular jetting means fewer backups during the dinner rush.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in East Honolulu

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. Trap primers get checked too.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in East Honolulu

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.
