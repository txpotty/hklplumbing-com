    Residential Water Softener Replacement in East Honolulu

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. Scale settles on the bottom of a tank heater and insulates the water from the burner, and tankless heaters clog even faster. A working softener stops that.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in East Honolulu, HI

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Hawaii homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Metered Softener Replacement in East Honolulu

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in East Honolulu

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Sometimes the whole unit goes.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in East Honolulu

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. Why soften water for the lawn?
