    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in East Honolulu

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in East Honolulu, HI

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps. Low flow with normal amp draw points to a plugged intake or a falling water level, which is a different repair. Testing first saves a pull.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in East Honolulu

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. We inspect the bladder, the switch and the tank connections. A properly sized tank can add years to pump life.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in East Honolulu

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. Testing starts at the surface. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in East Honolulu

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.
