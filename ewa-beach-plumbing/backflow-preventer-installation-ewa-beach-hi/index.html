    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Ewa Beach, HI

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Ewa Beach, HI, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Backflow Preventer Installation Cost Comparison in Ewa Beach, HI

    A residential pressure vacuum breaker is the most affordable option, while larger RPZ assemblies for commercial buildings cost a good deal more in parts and labor.     Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Ewa Beach, HI

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Ewa Beach

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.
