    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Ewa Beach, HI

    Yellow flame tips, soot on the burner door or a roaring sound at startup all mean the burner is not getting clean air. The burner comes out, the ports get brushed and the orifice gets cleared.     Tank Leaks and Anode Rods in Ewa Beach

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. Some rural homes in Hawaii rely on rainwater catchment, which needs good filtration and disinfection to keep tanks and fixtures in safe shape. We pull the anode, check how much magnesium or aluminum is left and replace it if the core wire is showing. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Ewa Beach home that has not started leaking from the shell. Water from the shell seam is another story. That tank needs replacing.

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Ewa Beach

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. No flame at all usually means a plugged pilot orifice or a bad piezo. Burner trouble looks different. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. Each part gets tested in that sequence.

    Bradford White & Rheem Gas Water Heater Repair in Ewa Beach, HI

    Bradford White and Rheem tanks make up a big share of what we repair in Ewa Beach, HI. Rheem gas models mostly use White-Rodgers or Honeywell controls with a status light. Bradford White uses its own sealed combustion design with a lint screen at the base and a thermal switch, and it needs a specific kit when the pilot assembly goes bad. We keep both on the truck.     Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Ewa Beach

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. We clean the screen and the chamber. A tripped thermal switch gets replaced.
