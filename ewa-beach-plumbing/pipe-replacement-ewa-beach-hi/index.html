    Burst Pipe Repair and Replacement in Ewa Beach, HI

    Frozen pipes are essentially a non-issue for homes in Hawaii, since sea-level and most residential areas never get cold enough to freeze water lines. Frozen pipes split when water inside expands. Exposed pipe in crawlspaces, garages and outside walls is most at risk, so we fix it and protect it from the next freeze. Then we test it.

    Drain Pipe Replacement in Ewa Beach, HI

    Drain lines fail too. Old cast iron rusts through. We replace cracked cast iron, failing ABS or old galvanized drains with PVC, and making sure vents and fall are correct. Camera first.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Ewa Beach

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is usually soft to moderately hard, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, choosing a layout that fits the house. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.
