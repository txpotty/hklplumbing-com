    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Ewa Beach, HI

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly.     PEX Pipe Replacement in Ewa Beach, HI

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

All joints use lead-free solder. Because some water chemistries eat copper, we look at your leak history before recommending it over PEX.
