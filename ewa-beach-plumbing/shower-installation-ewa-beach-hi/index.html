    How We Install Showers in Ewa Beach, HI

    New Construction Shower Installation in Ewa Beach, HI

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. Valves, supplies and drains go in at framing, with valve depth matched to the finish and drains set for your pan. Then the rough-in inspection. Trim goes on once tile is done.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Ewa Beach

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Ewa Beach, HI

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. When a pan is cracked or a valve is leaking into the framing, we pull the damaged shower, check the joists and subfloor and install a new unit as quickly as the framing allows. The framing must be dry. Then the new shower goes in.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Ewa Beach, HI

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. Layout comes first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Ewa Beach

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. We install heavy-duty commercial valves, push-button or metered controls when you want to limit run time, and drains sized for several showers at once. Accessible stalls get handhelds on slide bars. We phase the work to keep some showers running.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Ewa Beach

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. An electrician runs the circuit. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Ewa Beach

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. Replacing an old pan, converting a tub to a shower or swapping in a new valve and trim can make a big difference without tearing the bathroom down to the studs. You see each option's price. They tend to wrap up within a week.

    Digital Shower Systems in Ewa Beach, HI

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. An electrician handles the outlet. Our part is the plumbing.
