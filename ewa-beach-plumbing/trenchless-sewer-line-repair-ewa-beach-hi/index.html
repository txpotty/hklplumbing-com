    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Ewa Beach, HI

    Sewer lines in Ewa Beach, HI can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Some valleys and lowland areas have expansive clay that swells with heavy rain and can stress slabs, foundations, and buried pipes. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Ewa Beach

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.
