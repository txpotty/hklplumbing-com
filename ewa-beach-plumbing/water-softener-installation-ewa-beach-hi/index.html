    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Ewa Beach

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Ewa Beach

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. The savings add up. We install several models.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Ewa Beach

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Ewa Beach

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Ewa Beach

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Ewa Beach, HI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HKL Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.
