    A like-for-like tank replacement usually does not require a permit in most counties. Electrical changes or new piping may, and we check local rules for you.

    A new well pressure tank mainly helps maintain consistent water pressure and reduces pump cycling. However, if the issue is with the well pump itself or a clogged pipe, other solutions might be needed.

    Most pressure tanks last 10 to 15 years. Hard water, sand and frequent cycling can shorten that, especially when the pre-charge is never checked.

    Most pressure tank replacements take two to four hours. Adding a constant pressure system or multiple tanks can take most of a day.
