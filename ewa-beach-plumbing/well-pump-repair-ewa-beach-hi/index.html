    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Ewa Beach

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead.     Pressure Tank Repair in Ewa Beach

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it.     Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Ewa Beach

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Ewa Beach, HI

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. HKL Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.
