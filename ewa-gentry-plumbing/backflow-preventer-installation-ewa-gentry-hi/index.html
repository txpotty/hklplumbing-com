    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Ewa Gentry, HI

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Ewa Gentry, HI, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Ewa Gentry, HI

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Ewa Gentry, HI

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Frozen pipes are almost never a concern for homes in Hawaii, since sea-level and most residential areas never get cold enough to freeze water lines. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your local climate. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.
