    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Ewa Gentry

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are different. HKL Plumbing provides clear guidance and installation for both in Ewa Gentry. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, typically at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing.
