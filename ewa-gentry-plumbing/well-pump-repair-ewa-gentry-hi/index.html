    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. HKL Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Ewa Gentry operates at peak efficiency.
