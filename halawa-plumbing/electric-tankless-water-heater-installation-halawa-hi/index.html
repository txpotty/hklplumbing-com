    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Halawa

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is generally soft to moderate, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. A softener or scale filter helps, and the service valves we install make yearly descaling simple. It is a short service.
