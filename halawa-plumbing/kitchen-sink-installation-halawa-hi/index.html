    Workstation Sink Installation in Halawa, HI

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Halawa, HI

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Halawa

    Budget stainless sinks ring like a drum when dishes hit them or the disposal runs.     Same-Day Sink Replacement in Halawa, HI

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out cannot be patched for long. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, which makes same-day replacement common, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. Old stops come out too.

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Halawa

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.
