    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Halawa

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines.     Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Halawa

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. We have two ways in. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or tunnel under the slab from outside so the floors stay intact, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Halawa

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. There is often a grease trap or interceptor to work around.     Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Halawa, HI

    Diagnosis comes before price. Video tells us the problem and the sonde tells us the spot. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, then camera again so we are looking at the pipe itself. Guesswork costs money.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Halawa

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. Inspectors check all three in an open trench.
