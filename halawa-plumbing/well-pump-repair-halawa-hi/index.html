    Pressure Tank Repair in Halawa, HI

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. Fast cycling kills pumps young. The air charge gets checked and the bladder inspected, then the tank is recharged or replaced so the pump runs a full minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Pressure Tank Problems in Halawa

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. Precharge gets checked on an empty tank. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Jet Pump Repair in Halawa, HI

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Lost prime, seal leaks and impeller wear account for most jet pump calls. Our crew in Halawa restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for leaks that let air in. That matters most where water levels rise and fall through the year, since a dropping level can starve the pump and cost it prime.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Halawa, HI

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. A fault code on the drive points us in the right direction. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Halawa, HI

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. After that, we look at the capacitor and relay in the control box. We lock out the power before testing anything. All wiring and splices meet Hawaii electrical code before power goes back on.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Halawa

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow. Testing starts at the surface. If it has to come up, a pump hoist lifts the pipe safely, and we replace the burned motor, worn impellers or a bad check valve before setting it back.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Halawa, HI

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.
