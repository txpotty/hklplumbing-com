    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Hawaiian Paradise Park can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs.
