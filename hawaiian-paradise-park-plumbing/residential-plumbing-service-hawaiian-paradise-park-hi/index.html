    Slab Leak Location and Repair in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Some valleys and lowland areas have clay-rich soil that swells with heavy rain and can stress slabs, foundations, and buried pipes. We locate them with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. After that, you choose the repair. Some leaks get a spot repair, others a reroute overhead that skips the slab entirely.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Older homes may still have galvanized supply pipes or cast iron drains, both of which corrode faster in a warm, salty, humid environment. We repipe with PEX or Type L copper, running new lines through the attic and walls with small access holes, and the water stays on at night for most of the job. Expect two to four days.

    Drain Cleaning and Sewer Camera Service in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    Wipes are not flushable, whatever the package says. Kitchen grease, hair and wipes cause most slow drains, and for a main line backup we cable it open, then run a camera to look for roots, offset joints or a belly where water sits in the pipe. We send you the video file.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. We install and replace cast iron submersible pumps, check float switches and replace worn check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm.     Gas Lines for New Appliances in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI
