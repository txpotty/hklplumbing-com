    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule. The tech brings a replacement pump and a new check valve and can have water moving out of the pit within an hour of arrival in most cases. Then we find out why it failed.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    A pit that is filling with the pump silent means you have maybe an hour or two before water crosses the basement floor, depending on how fast it is coming in. Look at the breaker and the GFCI outlet, then pull the lid and check the float for a hang-up. A wet vac buys time. We stock the common pumps on the truck, and most emergency swaps are done on the first trip.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Most swaps take one to two hours. We also check that the float has room to travel without touching the basin wall.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Some insurance carriers ask for test records.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. New valve every time. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. Discharge goes away from the foundation. Test them each spring.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. Worn units get replaced, and we reset the float rod stops so the cycle is right. If your pit is wide enough, a submersible may serve you better.
