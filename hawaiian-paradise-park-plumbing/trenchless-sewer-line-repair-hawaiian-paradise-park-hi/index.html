    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line at high pressure to strip roots, grease and loose scale, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Pipe Bursting in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    Two pits are standard for a burst, one at the house end near the foundation and one out near the street or the city tap. Before pulling, the crew heat-fuses sections of HDPE into one continuous length on the ground. The winch or hydraulic puller then drags the bursting head and the new pipe through. It goes quickly.

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Trenchless Systems in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it.
