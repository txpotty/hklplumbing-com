    Electric Water Heater Installation in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. They do not need a vent. You get more spots to put it.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Venting is what keeps CO out. All work follows local code.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most.     Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Hawaiian Paradise Park

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.
