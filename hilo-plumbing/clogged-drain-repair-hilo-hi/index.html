    Complete Clogged Drain Repair Services in Hilo, HI

    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Hilo

    Kitchen drains clog from cooking grease, coffee grounds and starchy food that stick to the pipe walls and harden. Garbage disposals can push food further down the line where it collects in horizontal runs. We remove the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and branch line and clear the buildup back to the main stack. For heavy grease, we may follow with a small jetter to wash the walls clear.

    Floor Drain and Garage Drain Clearing in Hilo

    Floor drains in garages, utility rooms and commercial kitchens collect dirt, sand and debris, especially after storms track grit indoors. When their traps dry out from lack of use, sewer gas can also escape. We clear the drain, flush the trap and can install trap primers or trap seals to keep odors out.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Hilo, HI

    Bathroom sinks clog from hair, toothpaste and soap that bind together on the pop-up stopper and in the P-trap. Hard water can add a mineral crust that makes the pipe narrower over time. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line. We can also replace worn pop-up assemblies and corroded tailpieces during the same visit.

    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in Hilo

    Hair and soap scum are the most common causes of slow tubs and showers. We remove the strainer or overflow plate and pull the clog out with a drum auger designed for small diameter drains. If the drain is still slow, we check the trap under the slab or in the access panel. We take care not to scratch acrylic, fiberglass or porcelain surfaces.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Hilo, HI

    Washing machines discharge a lot of water quickly, and lint, fabric fibers and detergent residue build up in the standpipe and branch line. A clogged laundry drain often overflows at the standpipe mid-cycle. We clear the line with a cable and recommend a lint trap for the discharge hose. In some homes the laundry line ties into the kitchen line, so we check both.

    Preventative Drain Maintenance in Hilo

    Beyond clearing existing clogs, HKL Plumbing also provides preventative drain maintenance services. Regular cleaning, especially for homes dealing with mineral buildup from hard water, can significantly reduce the risk of future blockages. We can recommend and apply professional-grade drain treatments and schedule routine inspections to keep your plumbing system in good condition.

    Recurring Drain Clogs and Camera Diagnosis in Hilo, HI

    If the same drain clogs every few weeks, the problem is often a sagging pipe, a broken fitting or heavy scale. We run a small inspection camera through the line to see the inside of the pipe. You see the footage on screen with us. That lets you decide whether a deeper cleaning, a spot repair or a section replacement makes the most sense for you.

    Emergency Clogged Drain Service in Hilo

    A backed up drain right before guests arrive or during a holiday meal is not something that can wait. Our emergency technicians respond fast with full drain equipment. If several fixtures are backing up at once, we check the main line too, since that usually means the clog is further downstream.
