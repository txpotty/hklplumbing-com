    Commercial Water Heater Service in Hilo, HI

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different.     Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Hilo

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom.     Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Hilo

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Homes are commonly built on slabs or on post-and-pier foundations, and raised homes leave much of the plumbing exposed and easier to inspect. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Hilo

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage.
