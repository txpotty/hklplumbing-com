    Restaurant and Industrial Drain Service in Hilo

    Restaurants and industrial buildings put more grease and solids down the drain than any house. Interceptors need regular pumping. We jet kitchen lines between interceptor pumpings, clear floor sinks and trench drains, and keep a service log your health inspector can review if they ask about grease management.

    French Drain Installation in Hilo

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Hilo, HI

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send runoff straight into a garage or a door threshold. A channel drain catches that sheet flow. The channel goes into a saw-cut in the slab with a traffic-rated grate and solid pipe to an outlet.
