    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Hilo

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator.     Underground Gas Line Repair in Hilo

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen. Buried steel loses its coating to rocks in the backfill and then rusts through. Roots and settling do damage too. After an 811 locate, we excavate, cut out the failed run and lay polyethylene with a tracer wire beside it. Sand goes around the new pipe before backfill, per Hilo trench rules.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Hilo, HI

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. The bond is a heavy copper wire tying the gas pipe to the house ground. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.
