    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. It sets up like candle wax on the crown of the line.     Residential Grease Trap Repair in Hilo, HI

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, but some have one, usually a small plastic unit under the kitchen sink on a house with a septic system or a home bakery.     Baffle Repair and Replacement in Hilo

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Hilo, HI

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. A cracked lid lets odor out and lets mop water in. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. While the lid is off, we look at the baffles too.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Hilo

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. A cracked or loose baffle turns the trap into a pass-through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. Plastic units get a factory replacement part. Steel ones often need a fabricated baffle or a new trap.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Hilo, HI

    Lines downstream of the trap should stay clean if the trap is working. When we find grease past the outlet, the trap is not doing its job. If we only clear the line and skip the trap, it clogs again.
