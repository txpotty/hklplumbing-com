    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Hilo

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Hilo, HI

    A new sink changes the drain math. Deeper undermount or farmhouse bowls are common in remodels. A deeper bowl pushes the disposal lower, and if the trap arm in the wall was set for a shallow sink, the discharge can end up below it, so we sometimes lower the wall stub. We plan that during the remodel, not after.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Hilo

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube. A control box under the sink reads that pulse and flips the power, keeping live wiring well away from the water. If there is a spare faucet hole, we use it, otherwise we drill.

    Badger Series Installation in Hilo, HI

    The Badger line runs from the 1/3 HP Badger 1 up to the Badger 5XP. Every Badger shares one quick-lock mount. Because of that, a Badger-for-Badger swap can take under an hour, and the flange often stays if it is still clean. No cord comes in the box, so the cord kit is on us.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Hilo, HI

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. Expect the new unit to sit lower in the cabinet. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Hilo

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. The hose clamps onto the inlet and runs through an air gap or a high loop, which keeps dirty sink water from siphoning back into the tub. Both appliances get run together.
