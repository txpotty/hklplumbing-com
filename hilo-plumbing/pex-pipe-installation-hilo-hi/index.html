    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Hilo

    Low pressure at every faucet, not just one, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures.     Copper to PEX Conversions in Hilo

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Aggressive water, fast flow and soil contact under slabs can pit copper from the inside, and the first few pinholes are typically followed by more. Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is generally soft to moderate, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Hilo

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Hilo, HI

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. We carry the common sizes. Most emergency PEX repairs in Hilo, HI come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Hilo, HI

    A single bad section does not always mean a repipe. Once you are on the third patch, we will tell you straight whether the rest of the copper is heading the same way.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Hilo

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. After 40 or 50 years the bore is so choked with scale that a half-inch line delivers a trickle, and the water comes out tinted brown after it sits overnight. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Hilo

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool plus a ring slipped over the pipe end. We stretch the pipe and ring, seat the fitting, and within seconds the plastic shrinks back around the barb into a tight seal. No crimp tool to calibrate. Because the fittings are wide inside, each joint costs very little flow.
