    Slab Leak Detection for Frozen Pipes in Hilo

    Pipes under a slab rarely freeze, but the lines where they rise out of the slab at an exterior wall can. The riser is where trouble starts.     Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in Hilo, HI

    Finding the frozen spot takes the most skill. A thermal camera speeds it up. By checking which fixtures work and which do not, then scanning walls and ceilings with the camera, we can usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe and thaw it without cutting into the wall.

    Outdoor Faucet and Sprinkler Line Freezing in Hilo, HI

    Outdoor plumbing is the first to freeze, especially a hose bib with the hose left on. The hose traps water in the valve. Irrigation backflow preventers sit above ground with no insulation, and their brass bodies and test cocks crack easily, so we drain or cover them before the first freeze and repair them when they split.

    Pipe Insulation and Heat Cable Installation in Hilo, HI

    Insulation slows heat loss, but it does not add heat. For truly exposed runs, we add heat cable. We install self-regulating cable that adjusts its output to the pipe temperature, plugged into a GFCI outlet, and cover it with insulation so it uses less power and keeps the pipe above freezing on the coldest nights.

    Water Heater Line Thawing and Repair in Hilo

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in Hilo
