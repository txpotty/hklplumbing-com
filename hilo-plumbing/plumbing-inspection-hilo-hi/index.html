    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Hilo, HI

    Residential Plumbing Inspections in Hilo

    A full home inspection covers the system from the water meter to the sewer connection. Our plumber looks at all exposed pipe, runs every faucet and toilet and tests the heater, the main valve and the fixture stops. Pressure gets measured too. We note anything above 80 psi.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Hilo

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Our report tells buyers what pipe the house has, what the sewer looks like and how old the heater is, while there is still time to act. That leaves room to negotiate. Old galvanized, a damaged sewer or an aging heater can all become a repair request or a closing credit.

    Water Heater Inspections in Hilo

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Hilo, HI

    Warm temperatures all year mean water heaters work less hard, but moisture under sinks and in walls can lead to mold and rot if leaks go unnoticed. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. Outdoor faucets, exposed pipe, irrigation backflow devices and the heater drain and relief valve all get checked. Insulation gets checked too. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Hilo

    Around Hilo, sediment and fine volcanic grit can still clog aerators, valves, and water heater tanks, so periodic flushing is worthwhile. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Hilo

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. Backdrafting heaters can push combustion gases indoors, which is why we test the vent and report problems before we leave.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Hilo, HI

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Older homes are where we find the most issues, usually at appliance connectors that have been there since the house was built.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Hilo, HI

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.
