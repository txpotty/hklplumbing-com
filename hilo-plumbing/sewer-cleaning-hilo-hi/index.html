    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Hilo, HI

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Older homes may still have galvanized supply pipes or cast iron drains, both of which corrode faster in a warm, salty, humid environment. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting suited to older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a good option.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Hilo, HI

    Before any major sewer cleaning, especially for recurring issues, a sewer camera inspection is essential. HKL Plumbing uses waterproof cameras to travel through your sewer lines, providing a real-time view of the pipe's interior. This lets us accurately locate the exact cause and position of a blockage, identify pipe damage like cracks or collapses, and choose the most effective cleaning method, saving time and avoiding unnecessary digging in Hilo, HI.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Hilo

    Trees and shrubs send roots toward any moisture they can find, and a sewer joint is a steady source during dry months. Thirsty yard trees and shrubs are common causes in local yards. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out. Heavy root growth may need a follow-up with a jetter to clear fine root hairs. A camera inspection then shows where roots are entering so you can decide on lining or repair.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Hilo

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines carefully and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you plan a replacement.
