    Jetting a Sewer Line in Hilo, HI

    Grease and scale are jetting work. A cable only scratches them. A jetter nozzle with rear-facing jets pulls itself down the line and blasts the buildup off the pipe wall, restoring close to the full diameter of the pipe and slowing the next clog.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Hilo, HI

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. A crack stays a crack. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Hilo, HI

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Hilo, HI

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Copper was the standard for many years, while newer homes and remodels in Hawaii increasingly use PEX, which resists corrosion from salt air. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.
