    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Hilo, HI

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Some valleys and lowland areas have expansive clay that swells with heavy rain and can stress slabs, foundations, and buried pipes. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.

    Sewer Line Backups & Blockages in Hilo

    Water coming up in the tub when the washer drains is a classic main line symptom. The clog is past the branches. We clear it from the nearest cleanout, then run the camera to see what caused it, because wipes, grease and roots all call for different follow-up. Older Hawaii homes with clay lines often show roots right at the joints. We record what we find.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Hilo, HI

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    After-Hours Sewer Repair in Hilo

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. Our phones stay on. An after-hours call gets a tech with a cable machine and a camera, which is enough to restore flow in most cases and to tell you if you have a clog or a broken pipe. If it is structural, we schedule the dig for the earliest safe window. We avoid digging blind at night near gas and electric lines.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Hilo, HI

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Patching that line is wasted money. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your Hawaii property, with the footage, depth and surface restoration spelled out for each one. The right pick depends on depth and access.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Hilo, HI

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. Downtime costs money. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Hilo we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and give managers a clear window to share with tenants. We repair a lot of grease-clogged outlet lines.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Hilo

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Hilo, HI

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.
