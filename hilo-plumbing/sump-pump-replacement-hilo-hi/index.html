    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Hilo

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Hilo, HI usually ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any necessary pit changes. Battery backup systems will add to this cost, usually between $300 and $800. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your needs.

    Simple replacements typically do not require permits. New discharge lines or electrical work may, depending on your city.

    Sump pumps generally have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are crucial, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    In many areas, minor like-for-like sump pump replacements usually do not require a permit. However, if the replacement involves significant changes to the electrical system, discharge piping or the sump pit itself, a permit might be necessary. Our team will advise you on local code requirements in Hilo.

    It is a good idea to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne pumps. Cast iron models are preferred for durability.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or extensive discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.
