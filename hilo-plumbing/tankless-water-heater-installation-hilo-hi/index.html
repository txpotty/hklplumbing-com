    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Hilo, HI

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is generally soft to moderate, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.
