    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Hilo, HI

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Hilo

    Grit gets in. The small screen on the cold inlet catches sand and loose scale after a water main repair. Units with an air intake filter collect dust and lint, which can push the burner into a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Hilo, HI

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations.     Yearly Tankless Inspection in Hilo

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. We soap-test the gas connections and try the pressure relief valve. The air filter comes out and the igniter gets a look.     Annual Tankless Checkup in Hilo, HI

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Hilo
