    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Hilo, HI

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral commonly runs $4,000 to $12,000. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. Open digging can be cheaper on short, shallow runs. Once you add back concrete and sod, the gap usually closes, and we price both options side by side so you can see the difference.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Prep and Lining in Hilo

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line at high pressure to strip roots, grease and loose scale, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. A poor prep job means a wrinkled liner. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Hilo, HI

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.

    Sectional Point Repairs in Hilo

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. We push it in on a packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. One crack, one patch. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Upgrades in Hilo

    Bursting can upsize the line. We can pull in 6-inch HDPE behind a head that bursts an old 4-inch clay pipe, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. Each end only needs a pit around three by four feet. No trench in between.

    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Hilo

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet. The monitor shows us the pipe wall in real time, along with the footage counter, and a sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot on the ground above it. The locator also reads depth, which matters for pit planning. No guessing where to dig.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Hilo

    Point repairs usually cost $1,500 to $3,500. A full-length liner on a house lateral is often $4,000 to $12,000. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000, depending on the length of the run, how deep the line sits and how easy it is to reach both pits with a mini excavator. We price it after a camera look.

    Roots cannot get into the lined section. A properly installed liner has no joints, so they have nowhere to get in.

    Much of Hawaii sits on volcanic rock and lava, which makes trenching for sewer and water lines slow and sometimes requires special equipment. Soil that swells and shrinks with moisture puts stress on rigid pipe joints, which is how clay and cast iron lines crack and separate. HDPE flexes instead of breaking. A jointless liner helps too.

    Gurgling toilets, repeat backups, slow drains on the lowest floor and a sewage smell near the floor drain are the usual signs. A lush green strip in the lawn is another. Get a camera inspection first, since the camera shows whether the pipe is cracked, full of roots or simply clogged with grease that a jetter can clear.

    It handles cracks, fractures, root intrusion and small offsets well. Pipe that has crumbled away completely is a different case. Severe offsets and deep bellies may need a dig, since a liner follows the shape of the host pipe and will copy the sag right along with it. The video decides.

    Fifty years is the common design rating for cured-in-place liners and HDPE pipe. Many makers warranty materials for 25 years or more. How well the pipe was cleaned and prepped decides whether a liner reaches that number, so we record the prep on camera and keep the footage on file with your warranty.

    Workmanship and materials are typically covered for 10 to 20 years, depending on the product and the method. Manufacturers like Perma-Liner and NuFlow back their materials separately. Both warranties come to you in writing with the final invoice, along with the before and after camera video that shows what was installed and where.

    One to three days covers most jobs, compared with a week or more for a full dig. Lining a short lateral can finish in a single morning. Longer runs and bursts take more time, mostly for pit work and fusing the new HDPE pipe into one length before the pull. Cure time adds a few hours.
