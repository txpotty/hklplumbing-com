    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Hilo

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Hilo that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Hilo, HI

    Sewer lines in Hilo, HI can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Much of Hawaii sits on volcanic rock and lava, which makes trenching for sewer and water lines slow and sometimes requires special equipment. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Hilo

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Pipe Bursting in Hilo, HI

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.
