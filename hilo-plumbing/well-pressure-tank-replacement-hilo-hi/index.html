    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Hilo, HI

    A constant pressure system pairs a variable speed drive like the Franklin SubDrive or Grundfos SQE with a smaller pressure tank. The pump speeds up or slows down to hold a set pressure, so showers do not fade when a sprinkler kicks on. These systems cost more, often $2,500 to $5,000, but they end pressure swings. They also reduce hard starts on the motor. We explain whether your well and pump are good candidates.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Hilo

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A simple test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and verify the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Hilo

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is feasible or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more cost-effective and reliable option. Our technicians in Hilo provide an straightforward assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Hilo

    Many older systems have a tank that is too small for the pump, so the motor starts every time someone washes their hands. Tank size is based on drawdown, which is the available water stored between cut-out and cut-in pressure. For example, a 10 gallon per minute pump usually needs at least 10 gallons of drawdown for a one minute run time. That often means an 80 to 119 gallon tank rather than the 20 gallon unit that came with the home. Upsizing is one of the most effective ways to extend pump life.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Hilo

    Properties with livestock, irrigation or more than one home on a well need more storage than a single residential tank. We install multiple tanks manifolded together or large capacity models over 100 gallons. Proper sizing keeps big three-phase pumps from cycling too often. We also check the check valves and pressure ratings on the distribution lines. Work is scheduled to keep water downtime short for animals and occupants.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Hilo

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives accurate readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch. These small parts make a big difference in reliability.
