    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Kahului, HI

    Going from batch-feed to continuous-feed means adding a way to switch it on. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Kahului, HI

    A batch-feed disposal will not start until the lid is in, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. They are slower to use. Parents of young kids often think that is worth it.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Kahului, HI
