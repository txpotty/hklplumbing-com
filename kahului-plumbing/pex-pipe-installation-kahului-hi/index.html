    Why Choose HKL Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Kahului, HI?

    Manufacturer rules for PEX are specific: supports about every 32 inches on horizontal runs, a minimum bend radius, and no long sun exposure. Our crews follow them. Skipping them leads to kinks, sagging lines and fittings that strain, and it can void the pipe warranty the manufacturer would otherwise honor.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Kahului

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, but a different kind you drink from. Heating work calls for oxygen-barrier PEX, coated so oxygen cannot seep through the pipe wall and corrode the boiler, the circulator and the iron parts. Loops have length limits.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Kahului

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Kahului

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.
