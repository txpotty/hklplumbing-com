    Leak Detection and Repair in Kahului, HI

    Water stains on a ceiling rarely sit right under the leak. Much of Hawaii sits on volcanic rock and lava, which makes trenching for sewer and water lines slow and sometimes requires special equipment. Our techs use acoustic sensors and thermal cameras, then isolate lines with pressure tests, which lets us open one small hole in the right spot instead of cutting drywall all over the house. Repairs follow the same day when possible.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Kahului, HI

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Older homes may still have galvanized supply pipes or cast iron drains, both of which corrode faster in a warm, salty, humid environment. We repipe with PEX or Type L copper, running new lines through the attic and walls with small access holes, and the water stays on at night for most of the job. A typical repipe takes two to four days.

    Drain Cleaning and Clog Clearing in Kahului, HI

    Grease is the big one in kitchen lines. In bathrooms it is hair wrapped in soap scum, and we clear both with hand augers, sectional cable machines or a hydro jetter, depending on how far down the blockage sits and what the pipe is made of. A camera tells us if roots or a broken joint are behind a repeat clog.

    Drain Cleaning and Sewer Camera Service in Kahului

    Wipes are not flushable, whatever the package says. Most slow drains come down to grease, hair or wipes, and for a main line backup we cable it open, then run a camera to look for roots, offset joints or a belly where water sits in the pipe. We send you the video file.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in Kahului, HI

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. We size the pipe for the appliance's BTU load, run it in black iron or CSST, bond it where the code requires and pressure-test before the gas company or inspector signs off on the work. Then we light it and check the flame.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Kahului, HI

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. A gauge on the hose bib tells us your static pressure in a minute, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. Seized main shutoffs get replaced too.

    Water Softener and Filtration Service in Kahului

    Around Kahului, sediment and fine volcanic grit can still clog aerators, valves, and water heater tanks, so periodic flushing is worthwhile. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We put in and maintain whole-house softeners and carbon filters, including Kinetico and Pelican units, sized from a hardness test we run at your kitchen sink, not a guess. We program regeneration to your household's use.

    Repiping and Supply Line Replacement in Kahului

    Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning. It gets replaced with PEX or copper, same as rusted galvanized and pinholed copper, routing new lines through the attic and walls so the slab stays intact and the house keeps water each night. Drywall patches are included.
