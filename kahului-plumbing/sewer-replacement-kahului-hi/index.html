    Professional Sewer Replacement Services in Kahului, HI

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Kahului

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and eliminates corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Kahului, HI

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are prone to failure. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the extent of damage. Replacement ends recurring root problems.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Kahului

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It needs only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are completed in one day.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Kahului, HI

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench after inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Kahului

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent and nature of the damage. Minor cracks or isolated clogs might be resolved with targeted repairs, but widespread corrosion, multiple breaks, or a collapsed pipe often necessitates a full replacement.
