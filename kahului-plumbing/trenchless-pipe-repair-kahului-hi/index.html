    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Kahului

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Kahului yard.

    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Kahului

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Prep is half the job. Before lining, Picote heads mill out roots and scale, and after curing they reopen the lateral connections so each drain flows. A clean pipe holds a liner best.
