    Softener Loop Installation in Kahului

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Kahului

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. Our residential installs start with a test and a few questions about water use, then we pick a grain capacity that regenerates about once a week for your household. Weekly is the sweet spot. Every detail is handled on one visit.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Kahului

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Kahului, HI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HKL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Kahului

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Kahului

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Water Softener Replacement in Kahului, HI

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. Performance comes back right away.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Kahului

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. Bring your own unit. Programming is part of the job.
