    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Kahului, HI

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. A submersible pull and replacement runs about $1,800 to $5,000 and up, based on depth and horsepower. Replacing an aging pump during a pull saves paying for a second pull later. We lay out both options.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Kahului, HI

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. HKL Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Kahului

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Kahului, HI

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. Our technicians know them well, from the QD control boxes to the SubDrive constant pressure units. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Kahului, HI

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. High amp draw points to a weak motor. Low flow with normal amp draw points to a plugged intake or a falling water level, which is a different repair. Testing first saves a pull.

    Pressure Tank Problems in Kahului

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. We check the precharge with the tank drained. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Kahului

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. These pumps can also leak at the shaft seal or wear out an impeller. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.
