    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Kailua

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can often offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. HKL Plumbing can expertly install pedestal sump pumps in your Kailua home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Kailua, HI

    Power outages can happen, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We supply and install battery backup sump pump systems that switch on automatically if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Kailua, HI. Hurricanes reach Hawaii only occasionally, but strong storms can still cause power outages, flooding, and damage to outdoor plumbing and water heaters. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Kailua
