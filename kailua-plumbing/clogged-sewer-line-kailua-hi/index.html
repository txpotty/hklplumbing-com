    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Kailua, HI

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is vital for proper diagnosis and repair in Kailua, HI. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are typically isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at pinpointing the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.
