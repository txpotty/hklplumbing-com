    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes.     Residential Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kailua

    Residential electric tankless installs start with counting fixtures that run at the same time.     Rheem Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kailua

    Rheem's RTEX line covers most of the whole-home electric tankless units we install. The RTEX-18 runs on two 40 amp breakers, while the RTEX-24 and RTEX-27 need three, so we match the model to your panel before ordering. They mount flat on the wall. Water, temperature and leak checks finish the job.

    Residential Electric Tankless Heater Installation in Kailua

    Main water line size matters on electric tankless as much as panel size. A 3/4 inch line with good pressure feeds a whole-home unit fine, but a 1/2 inch line or low pressure can keep the unit from reaching full flow, so we check both before recommending a model.     Electric Tankless Water Heater Sizing and Selection in Kailua

    Sizing comes down to two numbers, flow rate and temperature rise. A 70 degree rise with a 27 kW unit gives roughly 2.6 GPM, enough for one shower and a sink. Warmer water means more flow. Panel and pipe layout matter as well.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless Electric in Kailua

    Switching from tank to tankless electric usually takes panel work and some re-piping. The higher amperage often means new breakers, heavier wire and sometimes a service upgrade, and the water lines get moved to meet the unit on the wall. We manage both trades. You get floor space back.
