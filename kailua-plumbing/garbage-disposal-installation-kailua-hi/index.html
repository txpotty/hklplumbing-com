    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Kailua, HI

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. They are common on islands.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Kailua

    On a batch-feed unit, the lid is the switch. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. That is the whole point. They are popular with families who have small children and people who prefer no open hopper in Kailua homes.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Kailua, HI

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.
