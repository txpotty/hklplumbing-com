    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Kailua, HI

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. The bond is a heavy copper wire tying the gas pipe to the house ground. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails inspection.

    Gas Line Repair & Replacement in Kailua, HI

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length. We take the joint apart, clean the threads and remake it with fresh gas-rated sealant. Cracked fittings get replaced. Where a length has rusted badly, usually near a damp crawlspace vent, we cut back to sound pipe and splice in new. Every remade joint gets a detector check and a pressure test.

    Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Kailua, HI

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. Testing runs from the meter inward, one section at a time. Drywall stays intact when possible. If the leak is inside a finished wall, running a new CSST line around it often costs less than opening and patching the wall. Nothing is turned back on until the gauge holds steady.

    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Kailua, HI

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising. We check both regulators, the one at the tank and the one at the house. Low spots get sniffed first. Your propane supplier owns the tank in most cases, so we coordinate with them for anything on the tank side of the first regulator. Rural properties with underground tanks get a line test too.
