    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Kailua

    Size and location are the main difference. A grease trap is a small hydromechanical unit, usually indoors under or near the sink. An interceptor is a large gravity tank, often 750 gallons or more, buried outside the building and sized for high-volume kitchens. Both separate grease the same way.

    Grease that escapes a broken trap hardens in the public sewer and causes sanitary sewer overflows. Overflow water can end up in storm drains and nearby streams. That is why pretreatment programs in Hawaii and elsewhere sample manholes for grease and fine the kitchens that keep discharging it. Repairs keep your kitchen out of that chain.
