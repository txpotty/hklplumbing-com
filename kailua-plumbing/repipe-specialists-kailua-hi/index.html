    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Kailua, HI

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Kailua

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Clogged aerators are a clue. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Commercial Repiping in Kailua, HI

    Commercial buildings in Kailua, HI often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. Our crew breaks commercial repipes into zones, working one riser or suite at a time while the rest of the building keeps water. Night work is possible. We use Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX sized from fixture units and installed to commercial code.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Kailua, HI

    Around Kailua, sediment and fine volcanic grit can still clog aerators, valves, and water heater tanks, so periodic flushing is worthwhile. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Kailua

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    PEX Repiping in Kailua, HI

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.

    Drain Line Repiping in Kailua

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    Copper Repiping in Kailua, HI

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, with lead-free solder or ProPress fittings. Copper takes a bit longer. Most copper jobs run a day longer than PEX, since each turn needs its own fitting.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Kailua, HI

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Insurers often ask about it at renewal.
