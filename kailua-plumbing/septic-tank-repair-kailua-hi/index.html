    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Kailua

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Every lid we set is screwed down or locked so a child cannot lift it.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Kailua, HI

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Kailua

    Sewage in the shower pan on a Sunday is an emergency, and we treat it like one. Our dispatcher asks if the tank has a riser at grade, because digging for a buried lid adds an hour to the call.     Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Kailua

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. An alarm going off means the water rose past the high-level float.     Effluent Pump Replacement in Kailua, HI

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.
