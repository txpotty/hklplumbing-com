    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Kailua

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Kailua

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. We see this most in older neighborhoods with clay pipe. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Kailua, HI

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Kailua, HI

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Both save driveways and mature trees. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.
