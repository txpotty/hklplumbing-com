    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Kailua

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Testing follows. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Kailua, HI

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. The membrane and slope get planned with the tile setter, so water reaches the drain instead of spilling out the open side.     Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Kailua

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Kailua, HI

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Either one protects against scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Shower Pan Installation in Kailua

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. We level prefab pans carefully. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in Kailua

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Kailua

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.
