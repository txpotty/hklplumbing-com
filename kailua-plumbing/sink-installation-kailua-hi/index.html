    Bathroom Sink Installation in Kailua, HI

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier.     Pedestal Sink Installation in Kailua, HI

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Kailua, HI

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Mounting the faucet and the pop-up on a sink that is still on sawhorses takes a fraction of the time and gets a tighter seal than working upside down inside the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Kailua

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job.     Commercial Sink Installation in Kailua, HI
