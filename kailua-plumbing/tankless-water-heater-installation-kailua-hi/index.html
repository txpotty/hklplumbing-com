    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Kailua, HI

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. The vent changes as well. We plan both on the quote.

    Cost-Effective Tankless Conversions in Kailua, HI

    A tankless conversion in Kailua, HI costs more than a tank swap, mostly because of the gas line upgrade, the new venting and the unit price itself. The savings come back over time. With no standby loss and a lifespan that can reach 20 years, many homeowners see the difference on the gas bill within the first few winters. Your quote shows the math.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kailua

    Gas tankless units in Kailua are the common pick for whole-house hot water because a single 199,000 BTU burner can feed two showers and a sink at the same time without losing temperature. The catch is gas supply. Most homes need the line upsized from the meter, often from half-inch to three-quarter inch or larger. We size it and pressure test it before the unit is lit.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kailua

    Sizing a home unit comes down to two numbers: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. That narrows the model list fast.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kailua

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Venting is usually two-inch or three-inch PVC. There is a condensate line to route as well.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Kailua, HI

    Upfront, a tankless install in Kailua, HI costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows.
