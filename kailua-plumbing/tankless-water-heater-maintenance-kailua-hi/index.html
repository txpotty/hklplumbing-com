    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Kailua

    The cost for tankless water heater maintenance in Kailua, HI generally ranges from $150 to $300, depending on the unit's age, condition and the extent of descaling required.     HKL Plumbing services all major brands of tankless water heaters, including Rinnai, Noritz, Navien, Bosch, State, Rheem and more. Our technicians are trained on the specific maintenance requirements and diagnostics for a broad range of models to provide expert care for your unit.

    It is recommended to have your tankless water heater professionally serviced at least once a year. In areas with very hard water, or for heavy usage, more frequent maintenance, such as every six to nine months, might help to prevent scale buildup and maintain efficiency.

    With regular descaling, many tankless heaters last 15 to 20 years. Neglected units in hard water can fail in under half that time.

    Common signs include reduced hot water flow, fluctuating water temperatures, strange noises coming from the unit, or error codes displayed on the control panel. If you notice any of these, it's a strong indication that your tankless water heater in Kailua is due for professional maintenance.

    A typical tankless water heater maintenance service, including flushing and inspection, generally takes 1 to 2 hours. The exact time can vary depending on the unit's condition, the amount of scale buildup and whether any minor adjustments or repairs are needed during the visit.

Mineral buildup can seriously restrict water flow and damage the heat exchanger, potentially leading to premature unit failure and expensive repairs or replacement.
