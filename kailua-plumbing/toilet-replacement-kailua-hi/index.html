    Standard Toilet Replacement in Kailua

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl.     What a Standard Swap Includes in Kailua

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected.     Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Kailua

    Expect roughly $350 to $800 for a standard, chair height or high-efficiency toilet installed, with the old one removed and hauled away. Rusted flanges and tight spaces push it higher. You get a firm number up front.

    Our short list starts with TOTO Drake, American Standard Champion, Kohler Highline and Gerber Viper, all of which flush well and use parts you can find at most supply houses. Mansfield makes a solid budget pick. The model gets matched to your rough-in and space.

    A handle that needs jiggling, a tank that refills on its own, a hiss after the flush or a flush that stops halfway all point to worn tank parts. Each of those parts can be swapped individually. Once a toilet is 25 years old, a full replacement usually beats rebuilding it.

    Some rural homes in Hawaii rely on rainwater catchment, which needs proper filtering and disinfection to keep tanks and fixtures in safe shape. Hard water leaves calcium scale in the rim jets and on the flush valve seat, which slowly weakens the flush and makes flappers leak. A fully glazed trapway resists scale better. We recommend models with fully glazed trapways and simple fill valves.

    Not for most straight swaps. Moving the toilet or reworking the drain usually needs a permit in Kailua, HI, and the inspector may want to see the work before the floor is finished. We tell you which case you are in.

    About two hours for a standard swap. That covers removal, flange inspection, setting the new toilet and testing it, with the old fixture loaded on the truck at the end. A bad flange adds thirty to sixty minutes.

    Watch for cracks, weekly clogs, a base that rocks after it has been reset, and a tank that uses 3.5 gallons or more. Age matters too. A toilet more than 25 years old is usually a good candidate even if nothing is broken yet.

    The toilet's outlet has to line up with the drain, and the rough-in tells you which models will do that without moving pipes. Measure from the wall, not the baseboard. Most are 12 inches. Ten and fourteen inch rough-ins need specific models.
