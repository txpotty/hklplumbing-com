    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Pipe Repair in Kailua

    Most residential trenchless jobs run $80 to $250 per foot. Length drives it. Diameter, damage type and the method all change it, so we scope first and write the estimate from the footage.

    Trenchless repair requires specialized equipment, materials and training. It should be done by an experienced contractor.

    We use liners and resins from established manufacturers such as Perma-Liner and similar systems rated for sewer use, chosen by pipe size and condition.

    CIPP lining jobs usually take one to three days. Bursting adds a little time. Pipe bursting may take slightly more, depending on length and access, but either one is much faster than open trenching, which can stretch to weeks once excavation and restoration are counted.

    Lining and bursting are rated for 50 years or more. The materials last. Both resist rust, roots and chemicals much better than old clay or cast iron, which explains the long rating.

    Cast iron, clay, PVC and concrete can all be lined. The pipe's shape counts. If the pipe has collapsed or shifted badly, bursting may suit it better than lining, and the camera tells us which.

    Many cities want a permit for sewer replacement, trenchless included. We check the local rules and file for permits when needed.
