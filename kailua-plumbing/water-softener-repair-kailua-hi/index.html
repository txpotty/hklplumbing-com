    Control Valve Rebuild in Kailua

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Kailua

    Scale on faucets means trouble. HKL Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, looks at each part of the system and gives you a repair plan for your Kailua home. A hardness test strip tells the story in seconds, and we run one before the repair and again after so you can see the drop in grains per gallon.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Kailua

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. HKL Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Kailua

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Resin Bed Problems in Kailua

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. New resin restores full capacity.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Kailua, HI

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Popular Water Softener Brand Repair in Kailua

    GE, Whirlpool, Culligan, Kenmore, EcoPure and Morton make the popular softeners we repair most often, along with the private-label units from hardware stores. Every brand has its own valve layout and weak points. HKL Plumbing knows those designs, so diagnosis is quick. Make or model, the repair in your Kailua garage gets done properly.

    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Kailua, HI

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor. We find the source, whether it is the bypass, the valve-to-tank seal or the drain connection, and replace the seal or fitting that failed. Then it gets a pressure test.
