    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Kailua

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. Small tanks give surprisingly little. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, and target a minimum one-minute run time. That often points to a bigger tank.

    Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Kailua

    Rapid clicking at the switch is the classic sign. That fast on-off pattern means the tank has no usable air, and the pump is now taking every hit of demand directly, which wears the pump and the switch contacts. We swap the tank and the cycling stops.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Kailua

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Kailua, HI

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. It is one of the most common tanks we install.     Constant Pressure System Conversion in Kailua, HI

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. Pressure stays steady even with two showers running. These systems can run on a smaller tank, but they cost more up front and need a compatible pump and drive. We explain the trade-off.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Kailua, HI

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. Air sits above it, water below. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, with short cycling, pressure swings and water spitting from the air valve. Replacement is the usual answer.
