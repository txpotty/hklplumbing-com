    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Kaiminani, HI

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Kaiminani

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.
