    Pinhole Leak Repair in Kaiminani

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that section.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Kaiminani

    PEX piping is a popular, flexible and freeze-resistant alternative to traditional copper or PVC, commonly used in newer construction and remodels in Kaiminani. Our team is skilled in PEX pipe repair, addressing crimp failures or accidental punctures. We also offer PEX pipe installation, a reliable, corrosion-resistant and cost-effective solution for re-piping projects that delivers a leak-free plumbing system for years to come.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Kaiminani

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in older homes and rusts from the inside until flow drops and leaks start at threaded joints. Repairs are possible, but every disturbed fitting can start a new leak. We replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric unions to stop galvanic corrosion. For homes with widespread galvanized piping, we will quote a full repipe so you can weigh both options.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Kaiminani, HI

    A single pipe repair is much less expensive than a whole-house repipe and makes sense when the rest of the system is in good shape. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can soon exceed repiping.
