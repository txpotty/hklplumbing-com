    Why Polybutylene Fails in Kaiminani

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is generally soft to moderate, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Kaiminani, HI

    Some homeowners in Kaiminani, HI want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, and join it with lead-free solder or press fittings. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.
