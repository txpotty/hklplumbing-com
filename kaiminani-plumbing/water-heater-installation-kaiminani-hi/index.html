    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Kaiminani, HI

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kaiminani

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Kaiminani

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Kaiminani, HI

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.
