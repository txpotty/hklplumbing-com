    Water Meter Leak Testing in Kaiminani

    The water meter is the first and simplest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere.     Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss.
