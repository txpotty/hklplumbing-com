    If you have had repeated leaks, it is worth pricing. PEX does not pinhole. PEX resists the corrosion and scale that cause copper pinholes and goes in quickly with fewer wall openings, so a PEX repipe often beats years of patching. We price both options.

    Water chemistry is a big factor. Around Kaneohe, sediment and fine volcanic grit can still clog aerators, valves, and water heater tanks, so periodic flushing is worthwhile.     Start with water stains on walls and ceilings. Look under sinks next. Green or blue corrosion on fittings, musty odors, a higher bill and the sound of dripping inside walls are common signs, along with weak flow at a single fixture. A visit settles it.
