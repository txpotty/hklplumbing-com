    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Kaneohe

    For a common 1/2 or 3/4 HP disposal, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink.     High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Kaneohe, HI

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model.     Same-Day Disposal Installation in Kaneohe, HI

    If your old disposal quits with a sink full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. We carry 1/2 and 3/4 HP units, mounts and cord kits on the truck, so there is no supply house run before your sink drains. Most swaps take about an hour.

    Disposal Installation in Rentals in Kaneohe

    Landlords want disposals that survive tenants. Our picks for rentals are durable models with easy mounts and decent warranties, and we can do multiple units in one trip during turnover. Quick installs cut vacancy days. We leave a care sheet for the tenant.

    Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in Kaneohe

    On new builds and remodels in Kaneohe, we coordinate with the general contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together. Plans usually call for a disposal circuit, a drain stub low enough for the trap and a dishwasher hose route with room for a high loop or air gap. Early planning prevents rework.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Kaneohe, HI

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. They are common on islands.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in Kaneohe

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. They need no wall switch, so they are a good choice for kitchens where adding a switch would mean cutting into finished drywall or tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Setup in Kaneohe

    On a batch-feed unit, the lid is the switch. We install the unit, then test the lid in every position to make sure the motor only runs when the lid is fully seated and stops the moment it is lifted out. It is the safety feature. Families with kids and anyone nervous about a hand near the hopper tend to choose these in Kaneohe homes.
