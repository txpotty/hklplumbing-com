    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    A disposal that grinds but drains slowly often has a clogged discharge tube or trap arm. Grease, rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We clear the line with the right tools and check for proper slope. We also explain what to keep out of the disposal.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Kaneohe

    A common issue with garbage disposals is a jam, often caused by hard food items like bones or fibrous vegetables.     Noisy or Grinding Disposal Repair in Kaneohe

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove debris. If the bearings are failing, the noise will continue. In that case, we can quote a less noisy replacement unit.

    Leaking Disposal Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed.
