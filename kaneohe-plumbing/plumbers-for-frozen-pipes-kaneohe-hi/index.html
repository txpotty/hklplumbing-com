    Costs range from about $200 for simple thawing to $1,500 or more for a burst pipe inside a finished wall. Where the pipe sits matters most. Open crawlspace lines are quick, and ceiling repairs take more time and drywall work.

    No pipe is freeze-proof. PEX handles a freeze best. It stretches instead of splitting, but its fittings can still fail, so placement and insulation matter most.

    A faucet that trickles or runs dry after a cold night is the most common sign. Visible frost on a pipe confirms it. A toilet that will not refill or a bulging pipe also points to ice.
