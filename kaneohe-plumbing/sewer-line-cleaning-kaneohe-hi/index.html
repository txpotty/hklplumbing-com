    What Our Kaneohe, HI Sewer Line Crews Handle

    Clearing Household Laterals in Kaneohe

    Most residential sewer clogs have three or four usual causes. Roots come first. Flushed wipes, built-up kitchen grease and root intrusion account for most backups, and we locate the problem on camera before choosing between snaking and jetting, so the fix lasts.

    Routine Sewer Line Cleaning in Kaneohe

    Routine cleaning clears slow buildup. Grease, paper and scale. We enter through a cleanout and run a sectional cable with the right blade for the pipe size, following with hydro jetting when the buildup is heavy, and most older lines do well with cleaning every one to two years.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Kaneohe

    A camera shows the line in real time. Problems become obvious. We look for low spots where water pools, offset joints, cracks, roots and corrosion, and a locator in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the surface, with a copy of the video for you.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Kaneohe

    Repeat problems call for a camera. It shows the cause. A sewer camera shows whether the blockage is a crack, a sag in the pipe or a root ball, which tells us the most cost-effective method and avoids unnecessary digging.

    Roots in Clay and Cast Iron in Kaneohe

    Older clay and cast iron invite roots. Joints are the weak spot. Roots seek out moisture and nutrients through tiny gaps, and we use root cutters and jetting to clear them from the line, then explain options to keep them from coming back.

    Jetting Heavy Buildup in Kaneohe

    Some lines need more than a cable. That is jetting work. A jetter cleans the entire pipe wall, which lasts much longer than a snake that only opens a path.

    Commercial Sewer Line Cleaning in Kaneohe, HI

    Commercial laterals see heavy use. Kitchens add grease every shift. Our larger cables and jetters handle commercial lines, and we work around your hours so customers are not affected.

    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Kaneohe, HI

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Replacement costs thousands. If the pipe is structurally sound, regular cleaning is often the better value, but if the camera shows a collapse or a long belly, cleaning only buys time, and you see both prices.
