    Precise Slab Leak Detection Services in Kaneohe, HI

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Kaneohe

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Kaneohe

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which specific line is compromised. This step-by-step approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, ensuring an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Kaneohe, HI.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Kaneohe, HI

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair Cost Comparison in Kaneohe, HI

    Slab leak detection typically costs $150 to $450. Direct access repairs usually run $1,000 to $3,000, including concrete patching but not flooring replacement. Reroutes generally cost $800 to $3,500 depending on distance and access. Full repipes cost more, often $4,000 to $15,000, but end the problem permanently.

    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Kaneohe

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the precise location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's particularly effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in Kaneohe homes.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Kaneohe

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak without risk or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Kaneohe, HI

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also look like slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is vital if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Kaneohe home.
