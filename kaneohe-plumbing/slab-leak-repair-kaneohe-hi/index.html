    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Kaneohe

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, locate the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks quickly helps protect your foundation.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Kaneohe, HI

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a sensible option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Kaneohe, HI.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Kaneohe

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    A major slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Prompt action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Slab Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Kaneohe, HI

    Spot repairs usually have a lower upfront cost but involve breaking concrete and repairing flooring. Reroutes cost a bit more but avoid concrete work and future leaks in that line. Whole-house repipes are the best choice when you have had multiple slab leaks or old copper throughout. We give you clear prices for each option.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Kaneohe, HI

    Whether your home in Kaneohe, HI has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are skilled in repairing both.
