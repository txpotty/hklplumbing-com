    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    Rinnai units are common, and scale causes most of their service calls. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush.     Navien Condensate and Fan Repair in Kaneohe

    Navien's E016 is a heat exchanger overheat, and scale or a backed-up condensate line is usually behind it.     Commercial Tankless Water Heater Repair in Kaneohe

    Restaurants and laundromats in Kaneohe often run two to six tankless units on a common manifold. We schedule around service hours when we can.

    Commercial Tankless Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    Hotels and gyms burn through hot water all day, so their tankless racks scale up two or three times faster than a home unit. We service rack systems with manifold kits, checking each heater's flow sensor and fault log separately, and set a descaling schedule by run hours. Quarterly is common.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Kaneohe, HI

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, so a code 11 on one unit means something else on another. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, but the tests behind them differ. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake. We clean the fan and burner, check the vent joints for gaps, and test the flow sensor's output against the spec sheet. Gaskets get replaced if they leak.

    Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. A rod coated in oxide reads low on a microamp meter and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, check the igniter for cracks or weak spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.
