    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Lining Systems in Kaneohe

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both systems are made to meet the ASTM standards written for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Kaneohe

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. Over time it softens, goes oval and blisters on the inside, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Vitrified clay that still holds shape lines well.

    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Kaneohe, HI

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints pull apart or crack. Homes are commonly built on slabs or on post-and-pier foundations, and raised homes keep much of the plumbing exposed and easier to inspect. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Upgrades in Kaneohe

    Bursting can upsize the line. A 4-inch clay lateral can be replaced with 6-inch HDPE, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. The pit at each end is small. No trench in between.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    Office and retail buildings often have a cast iron building drain under the slab that has rotted along the bottom.     Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Kaneohe

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. As it moves, it splits the old clay or cast iron and shoves the pieces into the surrounding soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. This is the fix for collapsed pipe.
