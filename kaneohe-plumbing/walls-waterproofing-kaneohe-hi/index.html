    Drainage System Integration in Kaneohe, HI

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Kaneohe

    Older homes may still have galvanized supply pipes or cast iron drains, both of which corrode faster in a warm, salty, humid environment. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Kaneohe, HI

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Kaneohe, HI

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Salt air and year-round humidity are the biggest stress on plumbing in Hawaii, corroding exposed copper, fittings, and outdoor fixtures faster than inland climates. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Kaneohe

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.
