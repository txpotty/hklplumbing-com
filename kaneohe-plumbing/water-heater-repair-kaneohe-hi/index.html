    Tankless Descaling in Kaneohe

    Navien, Rinnai, Noritz and Rheem tankless units show error codes on the front panel that help us narrow the problem quickly. Common causes are scale on the heat exchanger, a dirty inlet filter, a failed flow sensor or a weak igniter. Descaling uses a small pump and approved solution. Around Kaneohe, sediment and fine volcanic grit can still build up in aerators, valves, and water heater tanks, so periodic flushing is worthwhile.

    Gas Water Heater Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. Most no-heat calls trace to the thermocouple or the gas control. After any repair, every gas connection gets a leak test. Draft at the vent gets checked too.

    Relief Valve and Fitting Leak Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    T&P valves drip when the tank is overheating or the house pressure spikes, so replacing the valve alone may not stop it. We check the thermostat setting and the house pressure first. On homes with a pressure regulator, a missing expansion tank is often the real problem. Leaking fittings get redone.

    Electric Water Heater Element Repair in Kaneohe

    Lukewarm water or hot water that runs out in five minutes usually means a burned-out element or a stuck thermostat. Each element gets an ohm reading. Readings tell us whether an element is open or shorted, which explains the breaker trips. Parts for most tanks ride on the truck.

    Sediment Flush and Noise Repair in Kaneohe

    Popping, rumbling or crackling usually means a thick layer of sediment is trapping water and overheating the steel at the tank bottom. We drain the tank, break up scale with repeated fills and check the drain valve for blockage. A flush brings back lost capacity. Homes on hard water benefit from a yearly flush.

    Pilot Repair in Kaneohe

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Less often it is a dirty flame arrestor screen choking off combustion air. The thermocouple gets a millivolt test. If it reads fine, the gas control valve is next.

    Water Heater Brands We Service in Kaneohe

    Rheem, Bradford White, State and American Standard are the tank brands we see most, with Navien and Rinnai leading on tankless. Every brand has a part that fails first. We know those weak spots and stock the matching parts. On a Bradford White, for example, a tripped flammable vapor sensor can look exactly like a dead pilot to someone who has not seen one before.

    Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Kaneohe

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. We test each one, clean the burner and pilot assembly and check draft at the vent. A pilot that lights and then goes out a few seconds later usually points to a weak thermocouple or a clogged flame arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.
