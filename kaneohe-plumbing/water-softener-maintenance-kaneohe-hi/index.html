    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Kaneohe

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we check the condition of the resin beads and confirm the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to consistent soft water throughout your home in Kaneohe.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Kaneohe, HI

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Kaneohe, HI

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without squandering salt or water.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Kaneohe
