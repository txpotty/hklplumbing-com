    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Kaneohe, HI

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. High amp draw points to a weak motor. A clogged intake or a dropping water level shows up as low flow with normal amps, and that changes the repair entirely. Testing first saves a pull.

    Well Pump Brands We Service in Kaneohe, HI

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Myers make most of the pumps we pull in this area, and HKL Plumbing services all of them. Older units and newer variable speed systems are both familiar. Parts for the common models ride on the truck. A pump with life left in it is worth repairing, and that saves the cost of a new motor and the labor to set it.

    Pressure Tank Problems in Kaneohe

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. A waterlogged tank or a ruptured bladder causes most of it. We check the precharge with the tank drained. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets Hawaii code when the job is done.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. HKL Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Jet Pump Repair in Kaneohe, HI

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Common problems are loss of prime, a leaking shaft seal and a worn impeller. Our crew in Kaneohe restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for air leaks. That matters most where water levels rise and fall through the year, since a dropping level can starve the pump and cost it prime.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Kaneohe

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. A properly sized tank can add years to pump life.
