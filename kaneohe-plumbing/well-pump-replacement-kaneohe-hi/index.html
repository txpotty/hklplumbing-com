    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Kaneohe, HI

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. On a three-wire submersible, the start capacitor or the relay in that box fails far more often than the motor down the well does. Those get tested first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Kaneohe

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. Starters and overloads get replaced along with the pump. We schedule around watering times.

    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Kaneohe

    Pump and tank work as a pair. A tank that is too small or has lost its air charge makes the pump start dozens of extra times a day, and every start heats the motor. We check it on every pump job.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Kaneohe, HI

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    Well Pump Sizing in Kaneohe

    Sizing starts with the well log. The log, or a sounder down the casing, gives us static water level and well depth, and a draw-down test tells us roughly how many gallons per minute the well can actually give. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Kaneohe

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. Both use a small 2 to 20 gallon tank instead of a big one, since the drive keeps the pump running at a matched speed. Soft starts also cut wear on the motor. Expect to pay more up front.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Kaneohe, HI

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A few panels feed a DC pump through a controller while the sun is up. You store water, not electricity. We size panels to your daily gallons.

    No-Water Calls in Kaneohe

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. Usually the faucet spits air, then quits, after the motor has been limping along for weeks. Before we pull the pump, we test the switch and control box.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Kaneohe

    Most well pump replacements in Kaneohe, HI land between $1,500 and $5,000. The spread comes from well depth and horsepower, and from whether you need a new tank or wire at the same time. You get it in writing first.

    Expect 1 to 5 years on the pump. We back the installation with a labor warranty that covers our splices and pipe joints. Terms are in writing.

    Keep the tank precharge right and fix leaks fast. A tank low on air or a toilet that runs will keep the pump starting all night, and every start wears the motor. A sediment filter helps if your well makes sand.

    Low pressure, sputtering, no water or a spike in the electric bill. Frequent cycling is another warning.

    Shallow well pumps, usually jet pumps, sit above ground and pull water by suction from about 25 feet or less. Deep well pumps are submersibles that hang in the water and push it up. Physics sets that 25-foot limit.

    Our usual picks are Franklin and Grundfos for submersibles, Goulds for jet pumps, and Zoeller for some shallow well work. We can match an existing brand. We also work to your budget.

    Ten to twenty years is a normal life for a well-sized pump. Sand and constant short cycling both shorten it, sometimes to under five years on a well with an undersized pressure tank. Replace it when it fails.
