    Drain and Sewer Pipe Leak Repair in Kapaa, HI

    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Kapaa

    A sump pump lives in a pit cut below floor level and kicks on when a float rises. Stuck floats, worn motors and failed check valves cause most of the service calls we run on sumps. We test yours with a bucket. Add a battery backup if storms knock out power.

    Hydrostatic Pressure Management in Kapaa, HI

    Water pressing against the outside of a basement wall works into the weakest spot, usually the cove joint where wall meets floor.     Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Kapaa

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. A camera run from the sump inlet shows us where the tile is plugged. Jetting clears it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Groundwater Pressure Relief in Kapaa

    High groundwater pushes up through the slab as well as in through the walls. Water rising through floor cracks or around a floor drain during wet weather is the usual sign. Under-slab drain pipe tied to a sump lowers the water level beneath the floor. It is not a quick job.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Kapaa

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy adds strength. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Kapaa, HI

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Expect a few days of work. Our crew handles the footing drain and its outlet, and a waterproofing partner applies the membrane.
