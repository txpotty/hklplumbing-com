    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Kapaa, HI

    Fine grinding with slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. The trap comes off, the tube and arm get cleared with a properly sized cable, and we confirm the arm drops about a quarter inch per foot.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Kapaa

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.

    Emergency Disposal Repair in Kapaa, HI

    Water on the floor beats every other problem. Turn the disposal off, stop the dishwasher if it is running, and close the angle stops under the sink so the faucet cannot add more water to a backed up bowl. After that, call. Most times we stop the leak that day and either fix the disposal or price a new one on the spot.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Kapaa

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Most smells start at the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Kapaa

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Kapaa, HI

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Kapaa

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. Loose mounting rings rattle too. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Kapaa

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. After checking the chamber, snugging the mount and pulling debris, we run it empty to hear if the noise is coming from the motor. Bad bearings mean replacement.
