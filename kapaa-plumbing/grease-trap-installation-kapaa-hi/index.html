    What Goes Into a Grease Trap Install in Kapaa, HI

    Grease Trap Replacement and Upgrades in Kapaa

    Plastic and steel traps both wear out, just in different ways. With steel, rust eats the seams and the welded baffles first. Plastic cracks. We remove the old unit, set the new one in the same spot when it fits, and use no-hub couplings so future work is easier.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Kapaa, HI

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. They fit indoors, under a sink or in a floor pit. The flow control is not optional. Without it, a dumped sink pushes water through faster than the unit is rated for and the grease goes out the outlet. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Commercial Grease Trap Installation in Kapaa

    A commercial kitchen with a three-compartment sink, a pre-rinse station and two floor sinks can push 50 gallons a minute of greasy water at the dinner rush. Our sizing starts from your peak flow, the worst ten minutes of the night, rather than a daily average. Undersized traps are the usual failure. We set the unit, run the inlet and outlet in schedule 40 PVC or cast iron, and add the flow control fitting the manufacturer calls for.

    Grease Trap Installation for Schools and Institutions in Kapaa

    School cafeterias and hospital kitchens cook in big batches and wash in big batches, which means large pot sinks and a dish machine running hard for two hours after each meal. An outdoor gravity tank is the usual answer for that volume. We schedule around the calendar. School jobs fit into breaks, and hospital jobs get phased so meals keep going out.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Kapaa

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. Wastewater slows down in the tank long enough for grease to float and solids to settle. Sizing comes from your fixture units and the retention time your sewer authority wants, and we check that number with them before ordering a tank. No moving parts to fail.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Kapaa, HI

    Price follows size and location more than brand. The low end is a small indoor unit with easy access and a short pipe run. Outdoor tanks with digging and a permit sit at the top of the range. We quote after a site visit.

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. A trap 40 feet from the sink lets grease cool and stick in the pipe before it ever reaches the unit. Closer is better. We also check that a pump truck can reach an outdoor tank with about 100 feet of hose, since a tank the hauler cannot reach costs you more on every service.
