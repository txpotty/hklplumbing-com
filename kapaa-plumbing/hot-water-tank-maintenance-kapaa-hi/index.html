    T&P Valve Replacement in Kapaa

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. The new valve must match the tank's BTU or kilowatt rating.     Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Kapaa

    Street pressure over 80 psi is hard on every fixture in the house, and plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve above that number.
