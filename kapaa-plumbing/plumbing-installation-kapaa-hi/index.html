    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Kapaa, HI

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. We size every run from the appliance BTU ratings and total pipe length, and use black iron or CSST depending on the path. CSST must be bonded.     Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Kapaa, HI

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. The expansion tank takes up that swing so the relief valve stops weeping. Homes with a PRV usually need one. Fixtures below the sewer line get an ejector system with a sealed basin, a check valve and a vent.

    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Kapaa

    Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is usually soft to moderately hard, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places.
