    Effluent Pump Replacement in Kapaa, HI

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Kapaa

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. Bad lids get replaced, and risers bring the opening up to grade. Future pumping gets cheaper. Every lid we set is screwed down or locked so a child cannot lift it.

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in Kapaa

    Gurgling drains right after a pump-out point to the line between the house and the tank, not the tank itself. Roots and settling ground cause most sags and breaks in that run. We camera it from the cleanout and mark the bad spot on the surface. Then we dig one hole, not a trench. A second cleanout near the tank goes in while it is open.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Kapaa, HI

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. Our techs rebuild linear aerators and swap rotary ones, and we clear diffusers that have plugged with sludge. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Kapaa, HI

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Repair Versus Replace in Kapaa, HI

    Most septic systems that fail can be repaired for a fraction of the cost of a new one. You see the repair and the replacement priced next to each other, with our honest pick for a house like yours. A new field is sometimes the right call. Often it is not.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Kapaa

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. Each maintenance call includes a sludge and scum reading, and we pump when the solids reach about a third of the tank depth. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Septic Pipe Repair in Kapaa, HI

    Old homes may still have clay tile or Orangeburg pipe running to the tank, and both fail badly with age. Much of Hawaii sits on volcanic rock and lava, which makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower and sometimes requires special equipment. Shifting ground and roots crack the joints and flatten the pipe. We replace the bad run with 4-inch PVC, bedded in sand and sloped a quarter inch per foot. Short sections take an afternoon.
