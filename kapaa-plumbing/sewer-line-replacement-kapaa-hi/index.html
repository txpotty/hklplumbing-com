    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Kapaa

    Many older homes in Kapaa may have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to modern, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene). These materials resist corrosion, roots, and shifting soil, giving your sewer system a much longer lifespan.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Kapaa, HI

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may fix the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs much less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in good condition.

    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in Kapaa, HI

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their service life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is typically the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life. You get a smooth, root-resistant line out to the main.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Kapaa

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Kapaa

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, particularly along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that catches waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting options. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.
