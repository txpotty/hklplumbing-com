    How We Install Showers in Kapaa, HI

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Kapaa, HI

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. Slab homes need concrete work. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Kapaa

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Kapaa, HI

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Handles and the head come later. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Kapaa

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. The slope is our job. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Kapaa, HI

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves step up from there, holding an exact temperature and letting you set volume separately, which suits rain heads, handhelds and body sprays. Both prevent scalding. The outlet count usually decides it.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Kapaa

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Pair a rain head with a handheld and body sprays and a 40-gallon water heater may run cold halfway through. We check the heater. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Kapaa

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. We install heavy-duty commercial valves, push-button or metered controls when you want to limit run time, and drains sized for several showers at once. ADA stalls get handheld units. Work is phased so part of the room stays open.
