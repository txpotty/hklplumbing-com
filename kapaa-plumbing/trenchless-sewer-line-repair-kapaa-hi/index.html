    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Replacement in Kapaa

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. It goes oval and blisters, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Vitrified clay that still holds shape lines well.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Line Work in Kapaa, HI

    Restaurants feel a sewer failure fast, because grease and a dish machine put heavy load on a 4- or 6-inch building drain all day. We can line at night. A trench through a parking lot means lost spaces and a blocked entrance for a week or more. A liner installed from a cleanout skips all of it.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Kapaa, HI

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Ambient cure takes a few hours, and hot water or steam shortens it. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Lining in Kapaa, HI

    Smaller drain lines inside the house can be lined too, including 2-inch kitchen drains and the 3-inch cast iron stacks found in many older homes. We work through cleanouts and roof vents, so the drywall and tile stay where they are. Branch lines get reinstated with a robotic cutter. That saves opening a finished wall.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Kapaa, HI

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once inside, they branch into a mat that fills the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. With no joints left, there is nowhere for them to return.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Trenchless Repair in Kapaa

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet. The monitor shows us the pipe wall in real time, along with the footage counter, and a sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot on the ground above it. That tells us the depth. No guessing where to dig.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Kapaa

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. As it moves, it splits the old clay or cast iron and shoves the pieces into the surrounding soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.
