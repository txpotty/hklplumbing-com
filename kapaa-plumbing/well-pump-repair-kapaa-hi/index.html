    Control Box and Surge Repair in Kapaa, HI

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Windward areas get far more rain than leeward areas, so drainage, gutters, and sump planning vary a lot from one side of an island to another. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. An arrestor costs little.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Kapaa

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. Shaft seal leaks and worn impellers come next. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Kapaa, HI

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Kapaa

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. When the bladder fails, the tank fills with water and the pump starts and stops every time someone opens a faucet. We inspect the bladder, the switch and the tank connections. A properly sized tank can add years to pump life.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Kapaa

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. HKL Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Kapaa, HI well. Usually the electronics are fine.

    Power surges and worn parts top the list. Sand in the water and a bad tank or switch shorten pump life too.

    Submersible pumps commonly last 8 to 15 years, depending on run time, sand and water quality. A failing pressure tank that causes short cycling can shorten it badly.

    Electrical work around water is the main hazard. Heavy drop pipe and contamination risk make it worse.
