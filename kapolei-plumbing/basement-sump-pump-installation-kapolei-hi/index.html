    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Kapolei

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Kapolei, HI

    Power outages are common, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We supply and install battery backup sump pump systems that automatically activate if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Kapolei, HI. Windward areas get far more rain than leeward areas, so drainage, gutters, and sump planning vary a lot from one side of an island to another. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Kapolei, HI

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are powerful and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in Kapolei, HI will install your submersible pump with precision, making sure of proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, dependable operation, which matters most in finished basements.
