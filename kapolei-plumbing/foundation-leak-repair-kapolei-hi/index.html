    Water Line Reroute in Kapolei

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. No concrete gets broken. We usually run PEX because it bends around framing and does not corrode, and the drywall openings are cut small, often just a few squares at fixture walls, so patching goes fast.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Kapolei

    Locating comes before anything else. We split the system first. Hot and cold get tested separately, then we trace the losing line and walk it with the ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Kapolei, HI
