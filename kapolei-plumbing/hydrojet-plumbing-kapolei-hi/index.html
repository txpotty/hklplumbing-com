    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Kapolei

    The cost of hydrojet plumbing in Kapolei, HI can vary, typically ranging from $300 to $800, depending on the severity and location of the clog, as well as the length and diameter of the pipe. Complex jobs or main sewer line blockages may cost more, sometimes up to $1,500. We provide a clear estimate after an initial assessment.

    We utilize professional-grade hydrojetting equipment from reputable manufacturers like Spartan Tool, US Jetting, and Gorlitz. These brands are known for their reliability, power, and effectiveness in clearing even the toughest pipe blockages, ensuring a thorough clean every time.

    Most residential jobs take one to three hours, including camera inspections. Commercial lines with multiple runs can take a half day or more. We give you an estimate at booking.

    Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, usually leaving behind some debris. Hydrojetting, on the other hand, uses high-pressure water to thoroughly scour the entire inner surface of the pipe, removing buildup and leaving it clean. Hydrojetting provides a much more complete and longer-lasting solution.

    When performed by a trained professional, hydrojetting is generally safe for most types of pipes, including older cast iron or PVC. However, severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes might not withstand the high pressure. This is why HKL Plumbing always recommends a pre-service camera inspection to check pipe integrity before proceeding with hydrojet plumbing.

    Jetting removes the roots inside the pipe, but roots will grow back if the joint or crack remains open. Regular jetting can control them for years. Pipe lining or repair is the lasting fix.

    We use professional cart and trailer jetters from manufacturers like Spartan and RIDGID, paired with rotating, penetrating and root-cutting nozzles. Our cameras record video of the line before and after. This equipment lets us handle both homes and businesses.

    While hydrojetting provides a thorough clean, some underlying issues like severely damaged or collapsed pipes might cause clogs to return. If a clog reappears shortly after our hydrojet service, we will investigate further, often with another camera inspection, to identify any structural problems that might require repair or replacement, and discuss warranty options.
