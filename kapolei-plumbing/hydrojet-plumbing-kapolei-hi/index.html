    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Kapolei, HI

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Kapolei, HI

    A full sewer backup cannot wait for a weekday slot. First, we open the line. A cable opens it, then the jetter removes the grease, roots or wipes that caused it.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Kapolei, HI

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Kapolei

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers need drains that keep up with heavy use every day. A backup closes rooms or stores.     Root Removal Jetting in Kapolei

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Roots come back through the same cracks, so the camera shows where a repair or liner would stop them.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Kapolei

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Kapolei
