    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Kapolei

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Kapolei

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in many pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Kapolei homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Kapolei

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Kapolei

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs fast attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep partial water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.
