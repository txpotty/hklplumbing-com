    Locating and Repairing Slab Leaks in Kapolei, HI

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Kapolei

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. New line goes overhead. For older Kapolei homes, this works well with aging copper or galvanized pipe, since it bypasses every foot of buried line on that run. We keep drywall cuts small at fixture walls.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Kapolei, HI

    Direct repair is often the most practical fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Commercial Slab Leak Repair in Kapolei, HI

    Commercial slabs in Kapolei carry longer pipe runs and more branches. It means more line to test. We isolate branches one at a time, locate the leak and schedule cutting for after hours or a weekend, so your doors stay open and your customers never see a saw. Work meets Hawaii code and stays safe and compliant.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Kapolei

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Kapolei home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Kapolei, HI

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. Shut off the main. We help you close the main by phone, and a tech then isolates the leaking line so the rest of the house gets water back.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Kapolei

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold lines show no heat. For those, an acoustic sensor listens through the concrete for the sound of water escaping, and we mark the spot where it peaks, whether the house is an older ranch or a newer two-story. Both tools ride on every Kapolei truck.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Kapolei

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. With the mic catching the hiss through the concrete and the camera showing warm water spreading, we can usually narrow the leak to about a foot. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Kapolei home.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Kapolei, HI

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. Tunneling solves that. From a pit by the foundation, the crew hand-digs under the slab, shoring as they go, and your floors and cabinets never get touched. Many Kapolei, HI homes are good candidates.
