    Pipe Bursting Services in Kapolei, HI

    For badly damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for extensive issues in Kapolei, HI.

    Cost-Effective Underground Pipe Repair in Kapolei, HI

    Pipe Bursting in Kapolei, HI

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Kapolei

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Kapolei.
