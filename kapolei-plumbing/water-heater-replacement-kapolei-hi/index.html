    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kapolei, HI

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Kapolei, HI homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Kapolei

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Kapolei, HI

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.
