    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Kapolei

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard. Others notice a patch of grass that grows greener than the rest, or ground that stays soft long after the sprinklers shut off.     Main Water Line Replacement in Kapolei, HI

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patches on those materials rarely hold. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, usually in one day with a trench or a trenchless pull. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.

    Main Water Line Repair in Kapolei

    Your main line is the pipe from the meter to the house, usually 3/4 inch or 1 inch, buried anywhere from a foot to several feet down depending on frost depth. When it fails you tend to see wet ground, a spinning meter and weak pressure at every tap. We start by closing the house shutoff and watching the dial. A moving dial with the house valve closed means an underground leak. Then a ground microphone narrows the spot, and we dig one hole instead of a trench across your yard.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Kapolei, HI

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Kapolei, HI

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Fittings in the box loosen with ground movement and traffic. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, because the water provider owns the meter and everything on the street side. If it is ours to fix, we fix it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Kapolei, HI

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. Once one spot leaks, the pipe on either side is usually just as thin. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, and sometimes that buys a few good years. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. That fixes the pressure too.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Kapolei

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, and if it reads high we replace the pressure reducing valve with a Watts or Zurn Wilkins unit. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. That way you can actually shut the water off.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Kapolei

Then the new pipe gets pulled through underground. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, and most jobs wrap up in a single day.

    Look for a wet or extra-green patch in the yard. A hiss at the meter is a giveaway. Low pressure at every fixture, a bill that keeps rising and rusty or cloudy water can all point to a damaged line. The surest check is your meter. A moving dial means water is escaping somewhere.

    Green grass in one strip is a big one. Soggy ground in dry weather is another. You may also notice pressure dropping at every fixture, a rising water bill, cloudy water or a hiss near the meter. If you see two or more of these together, it is worth a meter test.

    Most jurisdictions in Hawaii require a permit for a full service line replacement. Spot repairs vary.     Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is generally soft to moderate, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places.     Type K copper, burial-rated PEX or HDPE poly. Each has its place. Copper resists punctures and lasts decades, PEX flexes with the soil and HDPE is what we use for trenchless pulls. We recommend one after looking at your soil, line length and budget.
