    Fleck Valve Programming in Kapolei, HI

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. We set it before we leave.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Kapolei, HI

    Resin beads break down after 10 to 15 years, faster on chlorinated city water, and the softener keeps regenerating while the water slowly turns hard again. Rebedding with new resin can be cheaper than a new system. The valve's age decides it. New resin under a tired valve rarely lasts.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Kapolei

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Kapolei, HI

    Swapping a salt-based softener in Hawaii homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Kapolei, HI

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Hawaii homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Kapolei, HI

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. Hardness gets tested on both sides of the unit, and the cheap fixes get tried before we talk replacement. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.
