    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Kapolei

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. A 20-gallon tank might give you five or six gallons. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, and target a minimum one-minute run time. That often points to a bigger tank.

    Pressure Tanks for Well Houses and Barns in Kapolei, HI

    Tanks in outbuildings face heat, cold and damp. Steel rusts fast in a damp well house. We often recommend a composite tank in those spaces, raise it off the floor on a stand, while protecting the switch and pipes from freezing. Simple protection.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Kapolei

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Threads on old tees weep. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, so we replace a crusted or corroded one rather than trust it. Whole set, one visit.

    Preventative Well Pressure Tank Maintenance in Kapolei, HI

    Check the air charge once a year. Shut off the pump, drain the system to zero, and put a tire gauge on the valve at the top of the tank, then add air with a small compressor if it has dropped. Water out of the valve means a torn bladder.
