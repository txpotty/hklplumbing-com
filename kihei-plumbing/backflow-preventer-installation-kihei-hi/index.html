    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Kihei

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Kihei. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HKL Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. In Kihei, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines.
