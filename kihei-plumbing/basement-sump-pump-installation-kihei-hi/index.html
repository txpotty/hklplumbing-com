    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Kihei, HI

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. That makes them quieter than a pedestal, and in Kihei, HI we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. Sealed wiring matters here.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Kihei

    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible.
