    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Kihei

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers.     Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Kihei

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. We see the most benefit in restaurants, schools and multi-family stacks.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Kihei, HI

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. When pinholes keep moving around the building, repiping the worst zones in Type L copper or PEX usually beats paying for more emergency visits. We map the system first. Work gets phased by floor or suite so tenants keep their water most of the day.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Kihei, HI

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks.     Backflow Testing and Certification in Kihei

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. We tag every passing device. The provider gets the report from us, and we keep a copy for your next test.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Kihei, HI
