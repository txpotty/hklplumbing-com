    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Kihei

    The cost of garbage disposal replacement in Kihei, HI typically ranges from $200 to $500, including the new unit and labor. This can vary based on the model chosen, its horsepower, and any necessary plumbing modifications. We provide a detailed quote upfront.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can often be repaired.

    To help it last, avoid putting fibrous or hard items like corn husks, bones, or coffee grounds down the drain. Always run cold water before and after use, and occasionally grind citrus peels to keep it fresh. Regular cold water flushing helps maintain the unit.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from two to ten years, and we guarantee our labor.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like garbage disposal replacement in Kihei does not require a permit. However, if significant plumbing modifications or electrical work beyond a simple swap are needed, a permit might be required. We will advise you if this is the case.

    We install InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen and GE. We help you choose based on power, noise and warranty.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on.
