    Grease Trap Brands We Install in Kihei

    Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura cover most of what we set in commercial kitchens, plus precast concrete tanks for the big outdoor interceptors. Each brand has its own lid gasket and baffle layout.     Food Truck Commissary and Ghost Kitchen Traps in Kihei, HI

    Commissary kitchens see a dozen trucks dumping wash water in the same two hours, which is a different load from a restaurant spreading it over a long shift. We size for that spike. Ghost kitchens with four or five brands under one roof often share a single trap, so the fixture count can be higher than the floor plan suggests. Each brand's prep sink counts.

    Gravity Grease Trap Installation in Kihei

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. Wastewater slows down in the tank long enough for grease to float and solids to settle. We size by fixture count and retention time, then match the tank to what the local pretreatment office for your building will accept. No moving parts to fail.

    Grease Trap Replacement and Upgrades in Kihei

    Plastic and steel traps both wear out, just in different ways. With steel, rust eats the seams and the welded baffles first. Plastic cracks. We remove the old unit, set the new one in the same spot when it fits, and use no-hub couplings so future work is easier.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Kihei

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. They cost more up front. They also need a dedicated electrical circuit, which we coordinate with your electrician before the unit arrives. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Kihei

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. We pipe the air intake the way the manufacturer and the code both require. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. A trap that sits out of level loses capacity, so we shim it flat before connecting anything.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Kihei

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. We count each sink, floor drain and dish machine that will tie in, then add the gallons per minute each one can dump. That number decides between an indoor unit and an outdoor tank, and where it can go with clean pipe runs to the sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    Residential Grease Interceptor Setup in Kihei, HI

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, though some do. We see them in licensed home kitchens and in houses where the sewer lateral keeps clogging with grease. A small 4 to 7 GPM unit under the kitchen sink catches most of it, and cleaning takes about ten minutes with a spatula and a bucket. Check with your local health office first.

    Plastic hydromechanical traps often last 20 years or more, while coated steel traps can rust out in 7 to 15 depending on how harsh the wastewater runs. Concrete tanks can last decades, though hydrogen sulfide gas slowly eats the concrete above the water line. Cleaning habits matter most. A trap that is scraped with a metal tool every week or left full of grease for months ages much faster than one that gets regular service.
