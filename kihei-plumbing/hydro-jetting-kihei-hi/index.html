    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Kihei

    Residential hydro jetting in Kihei typically costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    A thorough jetting often keeps a line clear for a year or more, depending on use and roots. Lines with heavy root intrusion may need maintenance or a repair.

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or caved in.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and wash debris away.

    For most homes in Kihei, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your needs.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are good reasons to consider jetting.
