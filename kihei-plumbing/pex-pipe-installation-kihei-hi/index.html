    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Kihei, HI

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Kihei

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. Give it four or five decades and the inside is so packed with rust that a half-inch pipe barely flows, and the first draw in the morning runs brown. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. Pressure comes back at the fixtures and the rust stops.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Kihei

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Kihei, HI

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to swap out the old pipe. While the studs are exposed we can run PEX back to a manifold or a sound trunk, set fresh stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a fridge line. An addition gets sized on its own. A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Kihei, HI
