    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kihei, HI

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Kihei

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We saw the floor, trench drains to the basin, set it, carry a two-inch vent to the roof and run discharge to the main drain through a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Kihei, HI

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours. A new system with a basin, slab excavation, venting through the roof, electrical work and an alarm costs more, but it is the only way to add a bathroom below the sewer line in most homes without a macerating unit. Ask for both numbers.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Kihei, HI

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kihei

    Residential ejector systems in Kihei serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Each system needs a sealed basin, a pump with a float, a check valve and a vent. The parts are simple. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kihei, HI

    Businesses in Kihei, HI with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. Commercial setups often run duplex pumps with an alternating panel, so one pump can fail and the other keeps the restrooms open. Closed restrooms cost business. Each pump can carry the load alone.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Kihei, HI

    Upgrading an old ejector does not always mean a new basin. If the basin is sound, we can swap in a better pump, replace the old swing check with a new one, add a full-port shutoff and install a high water alarm, which covers most of what a new system offers for a fraction of the cost. Cracked basins get replaced.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Kihei

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.
