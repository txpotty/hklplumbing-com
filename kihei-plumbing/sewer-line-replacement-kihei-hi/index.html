    How We Price Sewer Replacement in Kihei

    Length and depth drive the price. A short, shallow run through grass is a very different job from a long, deep one under concrete. Method matters too. We break out labor, material, equipment, permits and restoration on the estimate. No line items show up later.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Kihei

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. Sewer gas and moisture eat away the bottom of the pipe first, so the camera often shows a channel worn through along the invert even when the top still looks solid. Rough scale catches everything. Outside the foundation we replace it with PVC or HDPE. Under-slab cast iron gets a separate look at lining or rerouting.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Kihei, HI

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. We fuse the HDPE into one continuous length on the lawn. Most bursts finish in a day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Kihei

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. The crew trenches the full length of the lateral, removes the failed pipe and sets new PVC on compacted bedding with steady fall. Where we can, the sod is cut and set aside before the digging starts. The inspector sees open pipe. Backfill goes in compacted lifts so the trench line does not sink into a ditch a year later.

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Kihei

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. PVC and HDPE do none of that. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Kihei

    A winch pulls the bursting head. Because the head is a little wider than the old pipe, it cracks it into the surrounding dirt as the new HDPE follows. Only two pits get dug. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Kihei

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line first, often with a cutter and a jetter, then pull a resin-saturated felt tube into place and inflate it so it cures hard against the host pipe. It needs a pipe that still holds its shape. Lining shines under finished basements and slabs. We say plainly whether your line qualifies.
