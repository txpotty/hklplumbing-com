    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Kihei

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It needs only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are completed in one day.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Kihei, HI

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the right option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench once it passes inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Kihei

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent and nature of the damage. Minor cracks or isolated clogs might be resolved with targeted repairs, but widespread corrosion, multiple breaks, or a collapsed pipe often necessitates a full replacement. Our technicians in Kihei will conduct a thorough camera inspection to accurately diagnose the problem and recommend the most cost-effective and long-term solution. We provide clear explanations and transparent pricing for both options.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Kihei, HI

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, at the same time breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are well suited for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Kihei.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Kihei

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Kihei homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We thoughtfully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Kihei, HI

    Many older homes in Kihei, HI still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often recommend upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a significant upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.
