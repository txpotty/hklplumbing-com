    Standard Toilet Replacement in Kihei

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. At that point a swap makes more sense than another repair. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Kihei, HI

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush.     Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Kihei

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Kihei, HI

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Kihei

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then the leveling. We caulk the base except for a small gap at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Commercial Flush Valve Upgrades in Kihei

    Manual flush valves on older commercial toilets leak at the handle and stick open, and swapping to a sensor valve during a replacement improves hygiene without changing the rough-in. Battery and hardwired models are both available. We do the work after hours if needed, and we check that the new fixtures meet accessibility requirements.
