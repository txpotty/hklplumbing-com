    Why Choose HKL Plumbing for Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Kihei, HI?

    An expansion tank is cheap insurance for a water heater that costs much more. Skipping it can shave years off the heater, because every heating cycle on a closed system spikes the pressure and stresses the tank welds, the fittings and the relief valve. It is a common failure.

    • Prevent Leaks, Lower peak pressure cuts the risk of leaks and bursts.
    • System Protection, The tank absorbs expanded hot water, so your heater and pipes are not strained.
    • Water Heater Lifespan, Smoothing out pressure swings can add years to the tank.
