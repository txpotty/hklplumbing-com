    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Maintenance in Kihei

    A standard tank water heater flush and inspection in Kihei typically costs $100 to $250. Tankless descaling typically runs $150 to $300, and anode rod replacement adds about $75 to $200 including the part.

    With hard water, flushing once a year is recommended. Homes with very hard water or no softener may benefit from flushing every six months or so.

    Sediment buildup consists of mineral deposits, mainly calcium and magnesium, that settle at the bottom of your water heater tank. Around Kihei, sediment and fine volcanic grit can still build up in aerators, valves, and water heater tanks, so periodic flushing is worthwhile.     Popping or rumbling noises, lukewarm water, rusty or smelly hot water, and slow recovery times are typical signs.
