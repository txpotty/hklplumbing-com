    Jet Pump System Repair in Kihei, HI

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. HKL Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Kihei, HI

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps. Low flow with normal amp draw points to a plugged intake or a falling water level, which is a different repair. Testing first saves a pull.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Kihei

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. Precharge gets checked on an empty tank. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Kihei, HI

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Tropical downpours can drop heavy rain in a short time, and flash flooding in valleys can overwhelm drains and push debris into sewer lines. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. Arrestors are cheap.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Kihei

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. No water, low pressure or a pump that hums without moving water are the usual signs one has failed. We test the motor from the surface first. If it has to come up, a pump hoist lifts the pipe safely, and we replace the burned motor, worn impellers or a bad check valve before setting it back.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Kihei

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. The bladder, the switch and the tank fittings all get checked. Proper sizing adds years to a pump.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Kihei, HI

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Kihei

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. These pumps can also leak at the shaft seal or wear out an impeller. The pump gets reprimed and resealed, and the foot valve and suction line get checked, because a single air leak will undo the work. Everything is above ground.
