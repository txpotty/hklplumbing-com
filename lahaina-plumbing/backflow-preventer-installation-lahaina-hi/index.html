    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Lahaina

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Lahaina, HI

    For high-hazard applications in Lahaina, HI where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. HKL Plumbing is experienced with RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Lahaina

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Lahaina. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. HKL Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Lahaina

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Lahaina, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Lahaina, HI

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Lahaina, HI, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.
