    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Lahaina

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Homes are commonly built on slabs or on post-and-pier foundations, and raised homes keep much of the plumbing exposed and easier to inspect. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is usually soft to moderately hard, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can reduce these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.
