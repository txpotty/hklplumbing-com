    Reliable Shower Installation Services in Lahaina, HI

    Curbless Shower Installation in Lahaina

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproof layers.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Lahaina, HI

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are budget-friendly and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and guest houses. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or crack.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Lahaina

    Rain showerheads, body sprays and dual heads need careful planning for valve capacity and water pressure. We install thermostatic valves with volume controls and diverters to run multiple outlets. We also confirm your water heater can handle higher flow. The result is a spa-like shower that works the way it should.

    Shower Valve & Trim Installation in Lahaina, HI

    The shower valve is the heart of your shower's operation, controlling water temperature and flow. HKL Plumbing installs all types of shower valves, including pressure-balancing, thermostatic, and diverter valves. We also install new trim kits, including handles, spouts, and showerheads. Proper valve installation prevents scalding and provides consistent water temperature, a critical comfort feature for your home in Lahaina.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Lahaina

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Lahaina, HI

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more accessible shower.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Lahaina, HI

    For new homes and additions, we rough in shower valves, supply lines and drains during framing. Valves are set to depth for the planned wall finish, and drains are located for the chosen pan. We pass rough-in inspections and return to install trim after tile. Our scheduling keeps your build on track.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Lahaina, HI

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.
