    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Lahaina, HI

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. Slab homes need concrete work. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Lahaina

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. Valve, head, handheld and drain go in first, then the pan is sloped a quarter inch per foot to the drain. Testing follows. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Lahaina

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. The water heater gets sized up. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Lahaina, HI

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Lahaina, HI

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. Commercial installs get tamper-resistant trim and heads, plus drains sized for heavy simultaneous use. Scheduling fits your hours. A master mixing valve can protect the whole room from scalding.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Lahaina, HI

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. Valves, supplies and drains go in at framing, with valve depth matched to the finish and drains set for your pan. Inspection comes next. We return for trim after tile.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Lahaina, HI

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Lahaina

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. We set that slope. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.
