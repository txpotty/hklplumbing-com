    The P-trap under your sink is crucial for preventing sewer gases from entering your home and catching items that fall down the drain.     Fixing Loose or Wobbly Sinks in Lahaina

    Over time, sinks can become loose or wobbly, especially if they are undermounted or pedestal styles. This can be due to failing caulk, loose mounting clips, or even issues with the countertop. Our plumbers in Lahaina can re-secure your sink, making sure it's stable and properly sealed to prevent water infiltration and further damage to your cabinetry or flooring.

    Loose or Cracked Basin Repair in Lahaina

    Undermount sinks are held by clips and epoxy that can let go, leaving a gap and a leak along the counter. We will tell you plainly when a crack means replacement is the better value.

    Overflow and Drain Body Leak Repair in Lahaina, HI

    Water that appears only when the sink is full often comes from the overflow gasket or the drain flange putty. We pull the drain, clean the old putty or sealant, and reset the flange with fresh plumber's putty or silicone based on the basin material. Then we fill the bowl past the overflow and check for drips. This kind of leak is easy to miss and easy to fix once we find it.

    Supply Line and Shutoff Valve Repair in Lahaina, HI

    Braided supply lines and angle stops are rated for years, not decades, and the rubber inside breaks down in warm cabinets. A failing line can burst without warning and flood a bathroom. We replace worn lines with stainless braided connectors and swap stuck multi-turn valves for quarter-turn stops. It is a small job that prevents a very expensive one.

    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Lahaina, HI

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is typically to blame. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.
