    Sink Parts Replacement in Lahaina, HI

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Lahaina, HI

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Lahaina, HI

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Lahaina, HI

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Lahaina

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Lahaina

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees.     Kitchen Sink Repair in Lahaina

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. We fix the part that is wet. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.
