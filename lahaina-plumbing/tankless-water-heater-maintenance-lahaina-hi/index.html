    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Lahaina

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger.     Flush Now or Replace the Exchanger Later in Lahaina, HI

    A new heat exchanger can run $600 to $1,500 or more installed, and some manufacturers will not cover one that failed from scale. Descaling costs far less. Cared for, many units run 15 to 20 years. We can also tell you whether a softener or scale inhibitor would pay off in your house.

    Tankless Vent Inspection in Lahaina

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Lahaina

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Lahaina, HI

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Lahaina, HI
