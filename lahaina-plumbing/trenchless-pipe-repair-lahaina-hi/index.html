    Frequently Asked Questions About Trenchless Pipe Repair in Lahaina

    Most residential trenchless jobs run $80 to $250 per foot. Length drives it. Pipe diameter, the type of damage and whether we line or burst all move the price, so a camera inspection comes first and the written estimate from we follow from what the footage shows.

    Most cracked, rooted or separated pipes can be lined. Bellies may need more. A fully collapsed or deeply sagging line may need bursting or a dig, and the camera shows us which.

    Trenchless repair requires specialized equipment, materials and training. It should be done by an experienced contractor.

    Perma-Liner for lining and Picote for pipe prep. Both have long track records. We picked them because the liners cure into a structural pipe and the Picote cutters clean and reopen lateral connections accurately, which is where a lot of lining jobs go wrong.

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. Bursting replaces it. With CIPP, a resin-soaked liner cures against the old pipe wall, while bursting breaks the old pipe apart and pulls new HDPE into its place, leaving a completely new pipe.

    We use liners and resins from established manufacturers such as Perma-Liner and similar systems rated for sewer use, chosen by pipe size and condition.

    A properly installed CIPP liner can last 50 years or more. Often longer than the original. Since the liner resists rust, roots and chemicals, it often outlives the original pipe, especially clay or cast iron.

    Compared to digging, yes. Far less dirt is disturbed. Trees, plants and soil stay put, and since the old pipe stays as the host, little waste goes to the landfill.
