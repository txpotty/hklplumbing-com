    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Lahaina, HI

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Copper was the standard for many years, while newer homes and remodels in Hawaii increasingly use PEX, which resists corrosion from salt air. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. A rigid coating on soft brick traps moisture and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile. We repoint failed mortar joints before sealing.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Lahaina

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Lahaina

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes listed for continuous wet use, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them when we find a leak during demo. After-hours scheduling is available.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Lahaina

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Lahaina, HI

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while repeat drywall, cabinet and floor repairs add up fast, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill.     Crawl Space Encapsulation in Lahaina

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Salt air and year-round humidity are the biggest stress on plumbing in Hawaii, corroding exposed copper, fittings, and outdoor fixtures faster than inland climates. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also means less musty air upstairs.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Lahaina

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Lahaina, HI

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Salt air and year-round humidity are the biggest stress on plumbing in Hawaii, eating away at exposed copper, fittings, and outdoor fixtures faster than inland climates. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.
