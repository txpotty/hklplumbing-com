    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Lahaina

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely and efficiently. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Lahaina, HI

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Lahaina

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Lahaina

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit.     T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Lahaina, HI

    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Lahaina

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.
