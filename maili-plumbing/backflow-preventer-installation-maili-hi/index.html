    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Maili

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Maili, HI

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Maili

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Maili, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Maili, HI

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a straight path for chlorinated water to reach the supply. We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or remodeled.

    In Maili, most backflow preventers must be tested annually by a certified backflow tester to confirm they are working correctly and providing adequate protection. This is a separate service from installation but is essential for ongoing compliance and safety.

    Backflow preventers are usually installed as a preventive measure or because code requires them.
