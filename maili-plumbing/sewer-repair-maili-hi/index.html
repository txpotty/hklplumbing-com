    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Maili

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Maili, HI

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Maili, HI

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and locate it on the surface to keep the excavation tight. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, so nothing in the yard gets disturbed. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Maili

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. We record the whole run. Older homes in Maili often hide two or three problems in one line, and the footage shows every one.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Maili

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. Older homes often need this on at least one section. We stage the work so you lose sewer service for a few hours, not days.
