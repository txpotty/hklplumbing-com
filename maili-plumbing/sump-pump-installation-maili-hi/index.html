    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Maili

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Maili

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Maili, HI

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Maili, HI

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. Their 250 and 280 series cover most residential lifts, and the vertical float version fits narrow pits where a wide tethered float would rub. Parts are easy to find. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Maili, HI

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Maili, HI

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Access is often tight. The discharge exits through the rim joist and drains away from the house.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Maili, HI

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Backups add cost. We list each option on a written quote.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Maili

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.
