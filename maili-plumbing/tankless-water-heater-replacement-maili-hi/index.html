    We install Navien, Rinnai, Noritz, Takagi and Rheem tankless heaters. Each has strengths, and we recommend based on your flow needs, venting options and budget.

    DIY tankless water heater replacement is usually not recommended due to the complexity of gas lines, high-voltage electricity, water connections and venting requirements. Improper installation can lead to serious gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning, water damage or electrical hazards. It is best to hire a licensed and experienced plumber like HKL Plumbing.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, typically takes 4 to 8 hours.     Yes, you can absolutely replace a traditional tank water heater with a tankless unit. This is a common upgrade for homeowners looking for better energy efficiency and endless hot water.     Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail far sooner. Annual flushing makes the biggest difference.
