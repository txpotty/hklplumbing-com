    Smart Toilet Installation in Maili, HI

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord.     Commercial Restroom Toilets in Maili, HI

    Restaurants and offices get heavy-duty fixtures and accessible stalls built to code. Downtime costs a business money. We work with your GC on inspections.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Maili

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. We assemble them on the floor before setting. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Maili

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. We keep common two-piece toilets on the truck. Often the broken unit comes out and a 12 inch rough-in replacement goes in during the same trip, round or elongated. Close the stop until then.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Maili

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Maili

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Maili

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Maili

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.
