    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Maili, HI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HKL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Maili

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Maili

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them.     Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Maili

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Maili
