    Residential Backflow Devices in Makakilo

    Homes with irrigation or a fire sprinkler line often need a device to keep that water out of the municipal supply.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Makakilo

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. When pressure between the checks gets too close to supply pressure, the relief opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Makakilo. We install Watts and Febco units.

    Understanding Backflow Preventer Installation Costs in Makakilo, HI

    Device type sets most of the price, followed by pipe size and how much piping has to change. A residential irrigation PVB is a small job, while a 4 inch RPZ on a commercial main involves heavy valves, supports, a drain plan and sometimes an enclosure, so the two land far apart on a quote in Makakilo, HI. You get the price in writing.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Makakilo, HI

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Makakilo, HI. A spring-loaded air inlet opens when the pressure falls, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. Clearance and drainage get checked as well.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Makakilo

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks. It is the usual pick for low-hazard lines such as many fire sprinklers, and because it does not discharge water, it can go in a vault where local rules allow it, sized so it does not rob your system of pressure in Makakilo. Test cocks stay reachable.
