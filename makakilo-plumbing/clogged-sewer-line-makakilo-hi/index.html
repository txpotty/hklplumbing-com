    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Makakilo

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. It lasts much longer.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Makakilo, HI

    A sound pipe with a grease or root clog just needs cleaning. Broken pipe needs repair. Offset joints, cracks and collapsed sections keep clogging no matter how often they get cleared, so the camera footage decides whether you spend a few hundred dollars on cleaning or more on a spot fix, liner or replacement. We walk you through it.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Makakilo

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Makakilo, HI
