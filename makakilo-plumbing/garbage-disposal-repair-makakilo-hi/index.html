    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Makakilo

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A constant high whine means something else.     Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Makakilo

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most electrical faults are easy once found.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Makakilo

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Makakilo

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A bad solenoid or tripped overload in the panel kills the unit as fully as a jam does, but it gets fixed far faster.
