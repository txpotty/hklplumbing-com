    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Makakilo, HI

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Makakilo, HI

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Makakilo, HI

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it.     Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Makakilo, HI

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. We calculate the full load first. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Makakilo

Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.
