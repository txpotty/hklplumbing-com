    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Makakilo

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Makakilo, HI

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. The inlet tee sends flow down below the grease layer. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. We replace broken tees in Schedule 40 PVC and set the drop to the depth the code or the manufacturer calls for.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Makakilo

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. The run from the sinks to the trap is where grease cools and builds up. Jetting a belly buys time, but only regrading the pipe fixes it.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Makakilo

On old cast iron we lower the pressure and watch the camera so we do not open up a thin spot.
