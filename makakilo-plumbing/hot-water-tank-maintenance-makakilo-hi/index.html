    Standard Tank Tune-Up in Makakilo

    Burner and Pilot Service in Makakilo, HI

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. We pull the burner assembly and brush it clean.     Anode Rod Replacement in Makakilo, HI

    Water chemistry changes from one street to the next, and so does anode life. Most rods last three to five years. A softened water supply can chew through a magnesium rod in two.     T&P Valve Replacement in Makakilo

    Replacing a relief valve is a short job, but it has to be done right. The new valve must match the tank's BTU or kilowatt rating.
