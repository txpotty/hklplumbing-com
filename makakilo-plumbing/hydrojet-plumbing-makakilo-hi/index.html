    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Makakilo, HI

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Makakilo, HI

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.
