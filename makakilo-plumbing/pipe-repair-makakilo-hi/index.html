    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Makakilo, HI

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Both have weak points. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, and we use parts and methods made for each pipe type. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Makakilo

    Drain pipe made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. Stains give it away. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet, so we find it, swap the bad length and run water at full volume to prove it. Vents get checked too.

    Business Pipe Repair in Makakilo, HI

    Pipe trouble in a commercial building can close a business for the day. We keep you open. Our plumbers handle restroom groups, kitchen supply lines, mechanical room piping and larger diameter mains, scheduling the repair overnight or early morning when needed. We provide records for managers.

    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Makakilo, HI

    PEX and copper fail in different ways. Copper pins. Copper thins from the inside and develops pinholes, while PEX usually fails at fittings or where it was kinked during installation, so the truck carries tools and fittings for both. Each repair is pressure tested.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Makakilo

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Pressure fades over years. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, which makes full replacement of the affected run the smarter spend. Transitions get dielectric fittings.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Makakilo

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, so we replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric fittings and talk through a full replacement plan when the rest is close behind. The schedule is yours.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Makakilo

    A burst supply line can put several gallons a minute on the floor. Shut the main first. Frozen pipes are essentially a non-issue for homes in Hawaii, since sea-level and most residential areas never get cold enough to freeze water lines. We locate the split, cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with soldered, press or crimp fittings that match the existing line. Nearby pipe gets a look too.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Makakilo

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. One usually means more are coming.
