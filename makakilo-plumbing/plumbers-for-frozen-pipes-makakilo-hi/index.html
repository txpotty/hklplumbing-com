    A short, exposed section usually thaws in 30 to 60 minutes. Pipes inside walls take more time. A frozen line inside an exterior wall or under a floor can take a few hours, especially if it is PEX or CPVC, which we thaw with gentler infrared or warm air instead of an electric thawing machine.
