    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Makakilo

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. The bottom of a cast iron pipe goes first, and the camera will show a trench rusted through even when the top looks fine. Scale snags paper and grease.     How Pipe Bursting Works in Makakilo

    A winch pulls the bursting head. Because the head is a little wider than the old pipe, it cracks it into the surrounding dirt as the new HDPE follows. You get a pit at each end. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Makakilo

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. The crew trenches the full length of the lateral, removes the failed pipe and sets new PVC on compacted bedding with steady fall.     Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Makakilo

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. PVC and HDPE do none of that. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Makakilo, HI

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay is tougher but leaks roots. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.
