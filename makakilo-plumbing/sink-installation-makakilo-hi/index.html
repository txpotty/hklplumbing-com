    How We Set New Sinks in Makakilo, HI

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Makakilo

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Makakilo

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. A wall bracket lagged into blocking carries the weight, and if the wall has none, we open it and add a board. Anchors in drywall will fail. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Sink Installation Cost Factors in Makakilo, HI

    Labor is the main cost driver. Replacing old galvanized stubs or adding a second sink adds hours, while a same-size drop-in swap with good shutoffs is the cheapest job we do on any given week. We quote labor apart from the sink. You can buy it or we can.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Makakilo, HI

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Makakilo, HI

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. That does not work on undermounts. Those get plumbed from below.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Makakilo, HI

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. We fill the bowl to the overflow, pull the stopper, and watch every nut under the cabinet at full flow with a flashlight and a dry paper towel. Paper towels catch the smallest drip. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Makakilo

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Makakilo, HI

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.
