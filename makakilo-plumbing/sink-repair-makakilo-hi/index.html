    Hair Clog Clearing in Makakilo

    Burst Supply Line Repair in Makakilo

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Makakilo, HI

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. Pop-up linkage parts sit in a damp cabinet all day, and the pivot nut is the leak we find most often on bathroom sinks. We check pedestal and wall-hung sinks the same way. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Makakilo

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Makakilo

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Makakilo

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Makakilo, HI
