    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump.     Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Makakilo

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Makakilo

    Once a year is the minimum. We check the motor, impeller, float and discharge line on every annual visit. After that, we fill the basin and time the drawdown, since a slow pump-out is the first hint of a tired impeller or a restricted line.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Service in Makakilo

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. We still check the casting for cracks and the float for free travel. We carry the common Zoeller parts on the truck.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Makakilo, HI

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor.
