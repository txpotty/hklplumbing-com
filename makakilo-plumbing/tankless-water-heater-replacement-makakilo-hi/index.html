    Picking a Tankless for Your Makakilo, HI House

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Makakilo

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We still check sizing against the new model. Corroded service valves get swapped while we are there.

    Navien Venting and Install in Makakilo

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. We stay inside those limits. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Makakilo, HI

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Frozen pipes are almost never a concern for homes in Hawaii, since sea-level and most residential areas never get cold enough to freeze water lines. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Makakilo, HI

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. We check panel capacity and wire gauge before quoting.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Makakilo

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. Less drywall work that way. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Makakilo, HI

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. The catch is gas, since most tankless units need a 3/4-inch line or bigger plus new venting. Before quoting, we check the meter and pipe run, and afterward we cap the old flue and seal the roof. Expect a full day.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Makakilo, HI

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. You see both quotes.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Makakilo

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Swaps take an hour or two.
