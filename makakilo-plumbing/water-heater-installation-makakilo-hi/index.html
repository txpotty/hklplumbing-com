    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Makakilo

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Water heater swaps, full repipes, new fixtures, and any work tied to a cesspool or septic upgrade typically require a permit. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Makakilo. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Makakilo

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a good choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Makakilo, HI

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Makakilo, HI

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Makakilo, HI

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from surrounding air, which can make them efficient in warm garages and utility rooms. We install models from Rheem and A.O. Smith with proper condensate drainage. These units need adequate air space and clearance. They may qualify for utility rebates or tax credits. We help you decide if a hybrid is right for your home.
