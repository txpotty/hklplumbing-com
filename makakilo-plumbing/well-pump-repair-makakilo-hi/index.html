    Well Pump Brands We Service in Makakilo, HI

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Makakilo

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Makakilo

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. We check the precharge with the tank drained. Set it 2 psi under cut-in, which means about 38 psi for a 40/60 switch.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Makakilo, HI

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. We carry the common Franklin parts. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Makakilo, HI

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. Pulling and replacing a submersible pump can range from about $1,800 to $5,000 or more, depending on depth, horsepower and wire size. Replacing an aging pump during a pull saves paying for a second pull later. You see both numbers.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Makakilo, HI

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Air leaks on the suction side cause most failures. HKL Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. A jet pump that runs nonstop without reaching pressure has almost always lost prime or sucked air through a cracked fitting.

    Control Box & Electrical Repair in Makakilo, HI

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets Hawaii code before we restart the pump.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Makakilo, HI

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Hurricanes reach Hawaii only occasionally, but strong storms can still cause outages, flooding, and damage to outdoor plumbing and water heaters. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one.
