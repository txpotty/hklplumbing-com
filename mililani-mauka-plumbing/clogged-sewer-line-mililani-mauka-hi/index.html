    Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Mililani Mauka

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. Cabling alone is not enough.     Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Mililani Mauka, HI

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Not even close.
