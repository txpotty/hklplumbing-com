    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Mililani Mauka, HI

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Mililani Mauka

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. A new pop-up assembly goes in, matched to your faucet finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Mililani Mauka, HI

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. We replace the O-rings and tighten the mounting hardware, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Mililani Mauka, HI

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Mililani Mauka

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Mililani Mauka

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Only the very highest mountain elevations ever see frost, so winter plumbing prep that matters elsewhere generally does not apply here. We replace packing washers and seat washers on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Mililani Mauka

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. That waste lands on your water bill. The worn washer or cartridge gets tracked down and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Mililani Mauka

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.
