    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Mililani Mauka, HI

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Thin or cracked cast iron gets gentler settings or a lining recommendation instead.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Mililani Mauka

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Mililani Mauka, HI

    The lateral from your house to the street carries everything, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap. Settled low spots get extra passes, and we mark them on the video for future reference.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Mililani Mauka, HI

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Whole-House Drain Jetting in Mililani Mauka

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. The main gets jetted from the cleanout, and branches from vents or fixtures. Plan on two to three hours.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Mililani Mauka

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Mililani Mauka

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Mililani Mauka

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.
