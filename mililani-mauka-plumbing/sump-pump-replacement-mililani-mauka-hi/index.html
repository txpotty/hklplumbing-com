    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Mililani Mauka

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Commercial Sump Pump Service in Mililani Mauka

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Mililani Mauka, HI

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Mililani Mauka

    Hurricanes reach Hawaii only occasionally, but strong storms can still cause power outages, flooding, and damage to outdoor plumbing and water heaters. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. A high-water alarm lets you know the backup has taken over.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Mililani Mauka, HI

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. Worn units get replaced, and we reset the float rod stops so the cycle is right. A submersible is worth a look when the pit is wide enough.

    Choosing a Backup Battery in Mililani Mauka

We ask how often your pump runs in a storm and size the battery to match. Plan on a new battery every three to five years.
