    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Mililani Mauka, HI

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall.     Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Mililani Mauka

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles.     Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Mililani Mauka, HI

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan.     Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Mililani Mauka, HI

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries.     Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner.     Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Mililani Mauka, HI

Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.
