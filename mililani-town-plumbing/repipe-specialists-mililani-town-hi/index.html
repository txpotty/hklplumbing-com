    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Mililani Town, HI

    Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is usually soft to moderately hard, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. Water in many areas contains minerals that cause scale buildup inside pipes over time. This hard water scale narrows the pipe's diameter, reduces water pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in galvanized and older copper pipes. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral buildup, supporting a longer-lasting plumbing system.
