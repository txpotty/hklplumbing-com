    For standard sewer cleaning, such as snaking or hydro jetting to clear a blockage, a permit is typically not required in Mililani Town.     Most standard sewer cleaning jobs can be done within 1 to 3 hours. However, more severe blockages, such as those caused by extensive tree roots or serious pipe damage, may require more time and possibly additional services.

    Drain cleaning usually refers to clearing clogs in smaller branch lines leading from individual fixtures, like sinks or tubs. Sewer cleaning, on the other hand, addresses blockages in the main sewer line that collects wastewater from all household drains and carries it to the municipal system. Sewer clogs affect all drains, while drain clogs are usually isolated.

    While minor drain clogs can sometimes be handled with DIY solutions, main sewer line blockages require professional tools and expertise. Trying to clear a sewer line without proper equipment can worsen the problem or damage your pipes.

    Common signs include multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, foul odors coming from drains, and water pooling in your yard, especially near the sewer line. If you notice any of these in your Mililani Town home, it is time to call a plumber.

    Usually, standard home insurance policies do not cover routine sewer cleaning or damage from gradual wear and tear. However, if a sudden, unexpected event causes sewer damage, it might be covered. It is best to check with your insurance provider directly.
