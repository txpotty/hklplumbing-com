    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Mililani Town

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. We wash the air intake filter and check the flame rod for carbon. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Mililani Town

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Mililani Town

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Calcium and magnesium drop out on the hottest tube walls first and narrow the passages. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Mililani Town

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. We replace spent media. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Mililani Town, HI

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. We start a Noritz tune-up at the exhaust, checking for soot and any blocked termination. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Mililani Town, HI

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. The flue gets a look for obstructions. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.

    Same-Day Tankless Help in Mililani Town, HI

    A blinking error code and a cold shower usually mean the heater protected itself from something. Usually it is a blocked exhaust or heavy scale. Emergency calls get common parts and a descaling kit, and most heaters run again before we leave.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Mililani Town, HI

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. On a Rinnai an LC code means scale, on a Navien E003 means no ignition, and on a Noritz a 90 usually means an air or exhaust problem. We pull the code history before touching anything. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.
