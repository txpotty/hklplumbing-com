    Hydro jetting is recommended for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more thorough cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    Tree roots are drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines. During dry periods, roots will seek out any tiny crack or loose joint in older pipes to reach water, then grow and expand, causing significant blockages. This is a common issue for homes with mature trees.

    Water that drains slowly, gurgling sounds, sewer odors and water backing up into a tub or shower are the most common signs. If more than one fixture is affected, the problem is probably in the main line.

    Caustic and acid cleaners can damage older cast iron, soften plastic joints and harm the person using them. They also seldom clear the full clog. Mechanical cleaning is safer and more effective.
