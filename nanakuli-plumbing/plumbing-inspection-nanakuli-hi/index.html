    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Nanakuli

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Nanakuli

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Nanakuli, HI

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. It takes about an hour. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Nanakuli

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.
