    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Nanakuli

    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Repair in Nanakuli

    Most shower repairs, like a cartridge or diverter, cost $150 to $375. Valve replacements that require opening the wall usually run $450 to $1,200, depending on the valve and how much wall has to come open. Pan repairs cost more.

    Most cartridge and diverter repairs take one to two hours. Leak repairs that require opening a wall or rebuilding a pan take longer.

    Worn cartridge or seals, almost always. Hard water speeds that wear, which is why drips are so common, and a new cartridge with fresh silicone grease usually stops it for years.

    Every major brand, plus a lot of older ones. Moen, Delta, Kohler, Pfister, American Standard and Grohe make up most of what we see, and we carry cartridges for the common models. Discontinued brands sometimes take a day.

    Unscrew the head and soak it in vinegar first. If pressure stays low, the cartridge, the flow restrictor or a pressure regulator may need service.

    Showerheads are fair game for most people. Cartridges and anything behind tile are where DIY goes wrong, since a seized cartridge can crack the valve body. That is a common call for us.

    Watch for drips and temperature swings first. Weak spray, a slow drain, pipes that bang when you shut the water and ceiling stains below are all reasons to call. Early fixes cost less.

    Mineral scale in the head or the cartridge is the usual cause. A sticking balance valve or a partly closed main can also choke the flow. We test each.
