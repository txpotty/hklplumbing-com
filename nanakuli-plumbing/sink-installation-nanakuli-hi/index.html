    Each Kind of Sink We Install in Nanakuli, HI

    Undermount Sink Installation in Nanakuli

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Nanakuli, HI

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Restaurant and Office Sink Installation in Nanakuli

    Breakroom and prep sinks get used hard. Stainless bowls with commercial faucets go in strong enough for a full bowl and a stack of dishes. Downtime gets planned around your hours. Night installs are possible.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Nanakuli, HI

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. The bowl gets filled and drained while we watch every nut under the sink with a light and a dry towel. A towel shows a weep fast. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Nanakuli

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. Swapping a chipped drop-in for a new one in the same cutout usually takes a morning, and moving up to a deeper single bowl may only need a new trap and a short drain extension. New counters add a day. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Nanakuli, HI

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. An undermount on new stone costs more because of clips, cure time and a second trip to connect the plumbing, and a vessel sink adds a new drain and often a taller or wall-mounted faucet. Moving the drain adds the most. We price each piece on the quote.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Nanakuli, HI

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. We set the top in silicone, mount the faucet and drain first, and adjust the trap arm to meet the tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Nanakuli

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Old basket strainers fuse to the bowl, the disposal has to come off its mounting ring, and the dishwasher drain hose needs to be rerouted to the new disposal or tailpiece with a high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.
