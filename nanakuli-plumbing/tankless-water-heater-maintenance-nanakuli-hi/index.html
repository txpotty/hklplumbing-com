    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Nanakuli

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. The secondary exchanger produces acidic water.     Heat Exchanger Descaling in Nanakuli

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. A 45 to 60 minute circulation with descaling solution dissolves it.

    Noritz Descaling in Nanakuli, HI

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.

    Inlet Screen and Air Filter Cleaning in Nanakuli

    Grit gets in. A small screen at the cold inlet catches sand and scale flakes, especially after a main break. On units with an air intake filter, dust and lint load it up and the burner runs rich or throws a combustion code. We clean both on every visit.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Nanakuli

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.
