    Picking a Tankless for Your Nanakuli, HI Home

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Nanakuli

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. Newer Rinnai models usually fit the old mounting pattern. Less drywall work that way. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Nanakuli

    A cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board on an older tankless is usually not worth repairing past the ten-year mark. We match the new unit to your gas line, vent type and mounting spot whenever we can. Most swaps take four to six hours.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Nanakuli

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Most take under two hours.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Nanakuli

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Run two showers and the dishwasher together and you are near 8 GPM. That number picks the unit. Any gas, water or vent changes the new unit needs get done to current code.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Nanakuli

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Nanakuli

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Nanakuli, HI

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Nanakuli

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. A photo of the error code helps a lot. A leaking heat exchanger means replacement, usually done same day or next day.
