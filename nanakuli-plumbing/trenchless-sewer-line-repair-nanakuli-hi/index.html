    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Nanakuli, HI

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints pull apart or crack. Much of Hawaii sits on volcanic rock and lava, which makes trenching for sewer and water lines slower and sometimes requires special equipment. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.

    Sectional Point Repairs in Nanakuli
