    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Nanakuli, HI

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. We flood test the new pan before any tile goes down.

    Drainage System Integration in Nanakuli, HI

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Nanakuli

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Homes are commonly built on slabs or on post-and-pier foundations, and raised homes keep much of the plumbing exposed and easier to inspect. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Nanakuli

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Nanakuli, HI

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Nanakuli, HI

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Salt air and year-round humidity are the biggest stress on plumbing in Hawaii, corroding exposed copper, fittings, and outdoor fixtures faster than inland climates. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Nanakuli, HI

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. A lot of Hawaii housing dates from the postwar decades, including single-wall construction where pipes run through tight, cramped spaces. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. Old mortar joints get repointed first.
