    Pressure Switch Replacement in Nanakuli, HI

    Switch contacts pit and burn over time. Worn contacts can fail to open or close. When we swap a tank we look at the contacts and the sensing port underneath, since a new tank paired with a tired switch is asking for trouble. Cheap part.

    Galvanized Steel Pressure Tank Upgrades in Nanakuli

    Old galvanized tanks have no bladder at all. Water sits right against an air pocket. Over time the water absorbs the air, the tank waterlogs, and someone has to drain and recharge it every few months. A sealed bladder tank ends that chore.

    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Nanakuli, HI

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. The difference between two tank sizes is usually modest. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, and fewer starts is the biggest single thing you can do for pump life, so we often recommend it during a replacement.

    Well-X-Trol Pressure Tank Installation in Nanakuli, HI

    Amtrol's Well-X-Trol line uses a diaphragm design. It is one of the most common tanks we install. Its stainless or polypropylene water connection keeps water off the steel shell, limiting internal corrosion, and the factory pre-charge still gets checked and adjusted to your switch on install.

    Bladder-Style Pressure Tank Replacement in Nanakuli, HI

    A bladder tank holds water inside a rubber balloon surrounded by compressed air. The air pushes water out when you open a tap. Bladders crack with age, especially if the tank ran with low pre-charge, which waterlogs the tank. Replacement is the fix.
