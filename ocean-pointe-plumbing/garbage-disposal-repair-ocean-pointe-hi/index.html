    Disposal Repair in Ocean Pointe, HI: Symptom by Symptom

    Grinding Noise Repair in Ocean Pointe

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Ocean Pointe

    Jams usually trace back to one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Ocean Pointe

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. It is not a repairable part.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard are behind most disposal odors. Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is generally soft to moderate, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. Mineral scale traps the food. We clean the splash guard and chamber, check the drain for buildup, and replace a worn guard, which often clears up a smell that months of lemon peels never touched.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Ocean Pointe

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Most jams and small leaks are much cheaper than a replacement. Once a unit is eight to ten years old, leaking from the body or losing its motor, replacement usually wins, and we price both when it is a close call. You make the call.
