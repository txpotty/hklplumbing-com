    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Ocean Pointe

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. No flame at all usually means a plugged pilot orifice or a bad piezo. Main burner problems show up another way. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. Each part gets tested in that sequence.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Ocean Pointe

    Power vent heaters push exhaust through PVC with a blower on top of the tank. When one quits, it is usually the blower motor, the pressure switch or the hot surface igniter. Condensate pooling in a vent with the wrong slope can also trip the pressure switch, so we check the pipe run all the way to the termination before we sign off on a part. The control's error code is our starting point. Then each part gets a meter test.

    Bradford White and Rheem Gas Water Heater Repair in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Bradford White and Rheem tanks make up a big share of what we repair in Ocean Pointe, HI. Most Rheem gas units run a White-Rodgers or Honeywell valve with a blinking status LED. Using factory parts keeps your tank warranty valid.
