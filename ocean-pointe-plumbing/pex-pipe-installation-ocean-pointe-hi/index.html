    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Think of a manifold as a breaker panel for water. A central header feeds each fixture on its own line with its own valve, which lets you shut off one toilet and leave the kitchen running. You buy more pipe. The trade-off is almost no fittings hidden in the walls and steadier pressure when two showers run at once.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Ocean Pointe

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Either one lasts for decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
