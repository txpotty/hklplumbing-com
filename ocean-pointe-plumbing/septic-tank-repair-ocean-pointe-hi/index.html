    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. Once the outlet baffle is gone, solids head straight for the field. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Ocean Pointe

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. After pumping, we inspect the walls and the joints with a strong light, and dye flushed down a fixture shows us where the water moves. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    After-Hours Septic Backup Calls in Ocean Pointe

    Late-night backups usually start in the lowest drain in the house, often a basement floor drain or a first-floor shower. Our on-call tech carries a jetter and spare floats, which means most calls end with the drains working. Bad nights end with a pump truck. Either way, you know what failed before we leave.

    Septic Maintenance and Small Repairs in Ocean Pointe

    A sludge judge, a long clear tube, tells us how full the tank really is. Each maintenance call includes a sludge and scum reading, and we pump when the solids reach about a third of the tank depth. Filters get rinsed. Small cracks around the riser get sealed before groundwater finds them.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Some tanks have no riser, so we run a push camera through the small inspection port to see the baffles. That saves digging up the whole lid. A cracked or missing outlet tee means we dig at the outlet end alone, then install a PVC tee with a filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. Our techs rebuild linear aerators and swap rotary ones, plus clean out diffusers clogged with solids. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Septic Tee Repair in Ocean Pointe

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet can crack or get knocked off by the hose on a pump truck. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.
