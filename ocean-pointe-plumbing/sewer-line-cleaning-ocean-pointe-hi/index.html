    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Cleaning in Ocean Pointe

    Cabling a sewer line in Ocean Pointe usually costs $200 to $500. Jetting runs $400 to $900. A recorded camera inspection usually adds $150 to $350, depending on the length of the line and the access point we use.

    Sectional and drum cable machines, hydro jetters and color camera systems with locators from trusted professional brands ride on every truck.

    Drain cleaning covers single fixtures. Sewer cleaning covers the main. A sink or tub drain serves one fixture, while the sewer line carries all the wastewater from the house to the city sewer or septic tank, so a sewer clog affects every drain.

    Small drain clogs, maybe. Laterals need real equipment. Without the right machine and a camera, a DIY attempt often fails or damages the line.

    Slow drains in several fixtures, gurgling toilets, sewage odors and backups in the lowest drains are common signs of trouble.

    Water pooling in the yard over the line. Strong sewage odors too. Multiple drains backing up at once and gurgling from toilets or drains are the other main indicators that the lateral, not a single branch, is the problem.

    Root treatment products can slow regrowth after a mechanical cleaning, but they do not remove a root mass. Cleaning first is the step that matters.

    Rental machines can work on simple clogs, but they can kink or get stuck on roots. Pros add a camera check.
