    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.

    Tropical downpours can drop intense rain in a short time, and flash flooding in valleys can overwhelm drains and push debris into sewer lines.     Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Ocean Pointe

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. It is a different pump entirely.     Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Ocean Pointe

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. The check valve gets replaced at the same time.
