    Pipe Bursting in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. You'll see an entry and exit pit. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Ocean Pointe

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Ocean Pointe yard.
