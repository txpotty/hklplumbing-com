    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints pull apart or crack. Some valleys and lowland areas have clay-rich soil that swells with heavy rain and can stress slabs, foundations, and buried pipes. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under load.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Ocean Pointe

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. As it moves, it splits the old clay or cast iron and shoves the pieces into the surrounding soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. This is the fix for collapsed pipe.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Trenchless Repair in Ocean Pointe

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet. The screen shows the inside of the pipe live, and a sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot on the ground above it. That tells us the depth. No guessing where to dig.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Ocean Pointe, HI

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. The crew saturates a felt tube with two-part epoxy on a roller table at the truck, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Ambient cure takes a few hours, and hot water or steam shortens it. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Sectional Point Repairs in Ocean Pointe

    A point repair is a short liner, often 3 to 5 feet long, set over one bad spot. We push it in on a packer, guided by the camera footage, and the packer inflates to press the resin-soaked sleeve against the pipe wall until it cures. One crack, one patch. Sound lines with one bad joint are the right candidates.
