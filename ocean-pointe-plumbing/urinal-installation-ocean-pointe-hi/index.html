    American Standard and Kohler Urinal Installation in Ocean Pointe

    American Standard and Kohler make the china we install most.     Urinal Permits and Compliance in Ocean Pointe, HI

    New urinals usually need a permit. New fixtures mean rough-in and final inspections, and commercial jobs often need plans. Permits are on us. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. The carrier, 2 inch waste, 3/4 inch supply and vent all go in to the spec sheet before drywall. Inspectors see it open. Then the walls close.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. The volume matches the fixture rating. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Ocean Pointe

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Ocean Pointe

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Ocean Pointe

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. Replacing them with 0.5 or 0.125 gallon models on the existing carrier, and swapping the flush valve to match, cuts water use sharply in any restroom that sees regular traffic. Some utilities offer rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.
