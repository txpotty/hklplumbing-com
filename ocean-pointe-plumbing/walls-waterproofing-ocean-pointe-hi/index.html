    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Ocean Pointe

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall.     Interior Walls Waterproofing in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.

    Drainage System Integration in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Lasting walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Ocean Pointe, HI properties, so water is managed well and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Ocean Pointe, HI

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Ocean Pointe, HI
