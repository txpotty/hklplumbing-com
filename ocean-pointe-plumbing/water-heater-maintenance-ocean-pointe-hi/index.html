    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Ocean Pointe

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Ocean Pointe, HI

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats.     Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Ocean Pointe
