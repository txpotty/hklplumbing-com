    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Pearl City, HI

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Pearl City, HI

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Pearl City, HI

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Pearl City, HI

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Pearl City

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.
