    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Cleaning in Pearl City

    Neglecting commercial grease trap cleaning in Pearl City can lead to severe drain blockages, sewage backups, foul odors impacting your business, and potential fines from the local health department or wastewater authority. It can also cause costly plumbing emergencies and business downtime.

    Slow sink drains, foul odors, grease on the water surface near the outlet and backups from floor drains are common signs. If you notice any of these, schedule a cleaning right away.

    The frequency of grease trap cleaning depends heavily on usage. Commercial kitchens often require cleaning every 1 to 3 months, while residential units might only need it once a year or less. Local Pearl City health codes for restaurants might mandate specific intervals.

    HKL Plumbing services all common types of grease traps and interceptors, including passive hydromechanical grease interceptors (HGIs), automatic grease removal units (AGRUs), and large gravity grease interceptors (GGIs). We handle both indoor under-sink units and outdoor ground-level tanks.

    Yes, our technicians are familiar with all major grease trap brands and manufacturers commonly found in Hawaii, including but not limited to Zurn, Big Dipper, Schier, and Rockford Separators. We can service, clean, and often repair components for these systems.

    The terms are often used interchangeably, but 'grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor units with capacities over 500 gallons, while 'grease trap' usually describes smaller, indoor units under 100 gallons, such as under-sink models. Both serve the same function of separating FOG from wastewater.

    Grease trap cleaning removes fats, oils, and grease (FOG), along with food solids, sediment, and wastewater. This material is often referred to as 'brown grease' and requires specialized handling and disposal to avoid environmental contamination.
