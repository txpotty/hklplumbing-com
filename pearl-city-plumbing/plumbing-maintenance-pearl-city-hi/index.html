    Water Softener Service in Pearl City

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.

    Tank and Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Pearl City

    Mineral-heavy water shortens water heater life. We drain sediment, check the anode rod, inspect the burner or elements and confirm the relief valve opens and reseats, whether the unit is a standard tank or a tankless model on the wall. Most failures give warning. We spot the signs early.

    Hidden Leak Checks in Pearl City, HI

    A leak you cannot see still costs money. We shut off every fixture and watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic and thermal tools to track hidden leaks in walls, under slabs and in buried lines. Copper was the standard for many years, while newer homes and remodels in Hawaii increasingly use PEX, which resists corrosion from salt air. Catching it early limits damage. Usually the fix is minor.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Pearl City

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. Brine, injector, resin and regeneration all get checked, and we test the water at a tap. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Worn resin gets swapped.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Pearl City

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Hard water drops minerals that settle on the bottom of the tank, where they trap heat, waste fuel and make that crackling sound every time the burner or elements fire. We flush it out. Tankless units get a descaling flush through the service valves with a pump and descaler. Records support warranty claims.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Pearl City, HI

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. We check visible gas piping and appliance connectors for corrosion and leaks, confirm the shutoff valves turn, and note any old flexible connectors that should be replaced before they crack. Pipe material matters too. Older pipe types get flagged with repipe choices.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Pearl City, HI

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Pearl City, HI

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and grease buildup in the main and branch drains, which matters most in older homes with clay or cast iron lines that have been in the ground for decades. The recording is yours. If cleaning or repair makes sense, we explain the choices.
