    Water Softener Repair in Pearl City, HI

    Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is generally soft to moderate, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. White crust on faucets and spotty glasses are the first signs a softener has stopped working. Most of the time it is a salt bridge or a plugged injector. HKL Plumbing repairs the common brands and tests the water hardness before and after the fix so you can see the number drop. Resin lasts ten to fifteen years.

    Drain and Pipe Repair in Pearl City

    Hydro-jetting at around 4,000 psi clears grease and scale that a cable snake only pokes a small hole through, which is why the clog keeps coming back. HKL Plumbing follows every jetting job with a camera so you can see whether the pipe is sound. Damaged sections get cut out and replaced in whatever material matches the line. Jetting alone will not fix a broken pipe.

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Pearl City

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. HKL Plumbing repairs both, including the major brands, gas and electric. On a tank we check the thermocouple, gas valve, elements and anode rod. On a tankless unit, the culprit is often scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet filter, so we flush and clean before swapping parts.

    Gas Pipe Repair in Pearl City, HI

    Leave the house first if you smell gas, and call the gas utility from outside. Once the utility clears it, we handle the repair.
