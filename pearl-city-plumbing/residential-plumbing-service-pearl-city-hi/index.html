    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Pearl City, HI

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Copper was the standard for many years, while newer homes and remodels in Hawaii increasingly use PEX, which resists corrosion from salt air. Repipes are done in PEX or Type L copper, running new lines through the attic and walls with small access holes, and the water stays on at night for most of the job. A typical repipe takes two to four days.

    Toilet Repair and Replacement in Pearl City

    A running toilet can waste a couple hundred gallons a day. We replace flappers, fill valves and wax rings, and when the bowl is cracked or the toilet is older than the last bathroom remodel, we set a new water-saving model and level it on the flange. Rocking toilets get shimmed and caulked.

    Emergency Plumbing Repair in Pearl City

    Sewage in the tub at night counts as an emergency. The plumber shows up with a drain machine and a shop vac, clears the line, and checks whether the backup came from a simple clog or a main line that needs a camera and a real repair. You get both answers before we leave.

    Drain Cleaning and Clog Clearing in Pearl City, HI

    Grease is the big one in kitchen lines. In bathrooms it is hair wrapped in soap scum, and we clear both with hand augers, sectional cable machines or a hydro jetter, depending on how far down the blockage sits and what the pipe is made of. A camera tells us if roots or a broken joint are behind a repeat clog.

    Leak Repair and Slab Leak Service in Pearl City

    On a slab foundation, a supply leak can run for weeks unseen. Warm spots on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off or a water bill that doubles are the usual clues. We pinpoint it before quoting. Fixes range from a direct repair to rerouting the line overhead or a full repipe when leaks keep coming back.

    Water Heater Repair and Installs in Pearl City

    A water heater works hardest in winter, when incoming water is coldest. We work on gas, electric, tank and tankless heaters from Rheem and Bradford White, and the usual culprits are a heavy layer of sediment or a thermocouple that has quit reading the pilot. No hot water is often a quick part swap. New units get an expansion tank and pan when the code requires it.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in Pearl City, HI

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. Pipe size is figured from the BTU load, run it in black iron or CSST, bond it where the code requires and pressure-test before the gas company or inspector signs off on the work. Then we light it and check the flame.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in Pearl City, HI

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. Shower valves get rebuilt, cartridges replaced and tub spout leaks sealed, and if a fixture is too corroded to save, we install the replacement and haul the old one away so it is not sitting in your garage. A slow drip still adds up on the bill.
