    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. With no curb, the floor needs a steady quarter-inch-per-foot pitch to the drain and waterproofing that extends past the opening. We set that slope. Grab bar blocking goes in while the walls are open, so bars can be added later without hunting for studs.

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base.     Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. Linear drains help a lot. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.
