    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Pearl City

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale.     Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Pearl City, HI

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, and the same number can mean different things on two units. A Rinnai 11 is no ignition, a Navien E003 is ignition failure, and a Noritz 11 is also an ignition fault, yet the checks behind each one are not the same. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Pearl City

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that comes out of hard water as it heats up, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages. Around Pearl City, sediment and fine volcanic grit can still clog aerators, valves, and water heater tanks, so periodic flushing is worthwhile. We hook a small pump and hoses to the isolation valves and circulate descaler for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with clean water. The inlet screen gets cleaned too.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Pearl City

    Navien condensing units use a secondary stainless exchanger to recover heat from the flue gas. That makes condensate, and a plugged trap or a full neutralizer will shut the heater down with a drain or flue error. We clear the trap, check the PVC vent pitch and test the circulation pump on NPE-A models with built-in recirculation. Then we fire it at low and high flow.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Pearl City, HI

    Rinnai units are common, and most of their trouble starts with scale. The LC code is the unit's own warning that the exchanger needs a flush. We also see dirty flame rods behind a code 11 or 12 and tired combustion fans behind a code 61, and we test every one against Rinnai's published values. Parts come from the factory list.

    Tankless Flush & Scale Removal in Pearl City

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale. Most tankless installs include isolation valves with purge ports for exactly this job, and if yours does not, we can add them during the visit so future flushing takes less time. White vinegar will do in a pinch. Commercial descaler works faster.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Pearl City, HI

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. When a breaker trips under load, the cause can be a shorted element or a loose, scorched lug, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. Element resistance and supply voltage get measured. Scorched wire gets cut back.
