    Cost-Effective Bowl Swaps in Pearl City, HI

    Swapping only the bowl saves money when the tank is newer, the fill valve and flush valve are in good shape, and the bowl is still made in your color. White is the easiest to match. Discontinued colors are where bowl-only swaps fall apart, and then a full toilet costs about the same.

    Cracked Bolt Hole Repair in Pearl City

    Hairline cracks near the bolt holes often start when someone tightens the closet bolts too far, and they spread a little with every flush and every sit. Epoxy will not hold. The new bowl goes on with bolts snugged by feel, then finish with a quarter turn so the porcelain is not stressed again.

    Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Pearl City, HI

    Bowl-only swaps make sense when the tank is newer and the bowl is still made, since a matched bowl often costs $100 to $350 while a new basic toilet starts around $200.     Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Pearl City, HI

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. Look at it then or not at all. A cracked or sunken flange gets repaired before the new bowl goes down, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Cracked or Damaged Toilet Bowl Replacement in Pearl City

    Porcelain that cracks below the waterline leaks every time the bowl refills, and the water usually runs down inside the base where you will not see it for weeks. Even a thin crack will leak. We pull the bowl, check the flange and the subfloor, then set a matching bowl on a fresh seal.

    Old Toilet Upgrades in Pearl City, HI

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume.
