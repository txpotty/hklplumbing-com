    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Pearl City, HI

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Pearl City, HI homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Pearl City

    Electric water heaters in Pearl City usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Hot water is back the same day.

    Rheem and AO Smith Water Heater Replacement in Pearl City, HI

We help you compare.
