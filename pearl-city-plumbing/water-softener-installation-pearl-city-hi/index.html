    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Pearl City

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron adds to the setting. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Pearl City, HI

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. You will taste it. We install twin-tank setups or dual-stage units.

    Water Softener Replacement in Pearl City, HI

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. We remove the old unit and install a new metered model, then check the loop and drain line while everything is apart. Salt use drops. Performance comes back right away.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Pearl City, HI

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. We program yours to your water. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Softener Loop Installation in Pearl City

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Pearl City, HI

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Pearl City

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Pearl City

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.
