    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Pearl City, HI

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. HKL Plumbing repairs these units in Pearl City, HI. Media lasts a few years.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Pearl City, HI

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Fill the tank halfway.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Pearl City, HI

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Pearl City, HI

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Pearl City

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. The valve gets rebuilt with the kit made for it.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Pearl City, HI

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Pearl City, HI

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. Sound tank and valve, repair wins. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.
