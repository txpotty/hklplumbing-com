    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Royal Kunia

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. Dirt falls in through the crack. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded stainless couplings rated for the transition between materials, then test the repair with water and video before closing the hole. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Royal Kunia

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Royal Kunia, HI

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Royal Kunia, HI

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. A closed kitchen loses revenue by the hour.     After-Hours Sewer Repair in Royal Kunia

    Main lines like to fail on holidays. We still answer.
