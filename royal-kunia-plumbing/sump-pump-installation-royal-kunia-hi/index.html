    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Royal Kunia

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Royal Kunia

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin.     Primary Sump Pump Installation in Royal Kunia

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Royal Kunia

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume.     Sump Pump Installation Cost in Royal Kunia, HI

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Backups add cost. Your written quote shows each option and its price.
