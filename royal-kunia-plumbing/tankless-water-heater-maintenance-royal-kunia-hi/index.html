    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Royal Kunia

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Royal Kunia, HI

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. We check gas pressure with all units firing at once. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Royal Kunia

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Royal Kunia, HI

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Next comes the combustion air intake and the fan. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Royal Kunia

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. We replace spent media. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Royal Kunia, HI

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. You get a written report with the readings, so next year's visit has something to compare against.
