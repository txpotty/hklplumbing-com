    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Royal Kunia

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet usually runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a firm price up front.

    HKL Plumbing stands behind our workmanship. We offer a warranty on all our toilet installations, covering labor and making sure your new toilet works as expected. Specific warranty details will be provided at the time of service.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and simpler cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are widely available and perform well.

    Common signs you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, cracks in the porcelain bowl or tank, constant running water, a wobbly base, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. Upgrading can save water and refresh the look of your bathroom.

    Replacing a toilet in the same location usually does not need a permit, but new bathrooms, additions and drain relocations typically do. Requirements vary by city, and we check with the local building department for your project.

    Both work when the flange is set correctly. Wax-free foam or rubber seals are more forgiving if the toilet needs repositioning and hold up well in warm bathrooms. We choose based on your flange height and flooring.

    A standard toilet installation, assuming no major plumbing modifications are needed, usually takes about 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new rough-ins or dealing with damaged subflooring, can take longer.
