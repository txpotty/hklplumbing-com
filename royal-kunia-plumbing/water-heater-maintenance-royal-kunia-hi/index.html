    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Royal Kunia, HI

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We test the valve, confirm it reseats and check that the discharge pipe is routed correctly. Leaky valves get replaced. We check the expansion tank too.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Royal Kunia

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Royal Kunia, HI

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. We flush it with descaler. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Yearly Service Plans in Royal Kunia, HI

    Forgetting is the main reason tanks go unflushed. Plans schedule the visit, send reminders and add priority booking and repair discounts. We track it for you. Long-term savings add up.

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Royal Kunia, HI

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater.     Anode Rod Replacement in Royal Kunia

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Water chemistry can wear a rod out in a few years, so we check it every time. We swap worn ones. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.

    Anode Rod Checks in Royal Kunia
