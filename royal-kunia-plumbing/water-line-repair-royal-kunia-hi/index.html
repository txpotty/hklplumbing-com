    A full service line replacement usually needs a plumbing permit. Spot repairs are often exempt. The rules come from your city or county, and they can differ even between neighboring towns in the same county. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection when it is required.

    A loose fitting on an exposed hose bib, sure. The buried main is different. You need the leak located, utility lines marked, a safe excavation and burial-rated fittings under the Hawaii plumbing code. A bad joint underground means digging the same hole twice. Most homeowners hire it out.

    You can tighten an exposed fitting at a hose bib. Buried repairs are not a weekend project. They need the leak located, 811 marking, safe digging around the gas and power lines nearby and code-approved fittings made up correctly. A mistake means a bigger leak and the same hole dug twice.
