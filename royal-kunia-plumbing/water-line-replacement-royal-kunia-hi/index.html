    PEX Water Line Installation in Royal Kunia, HI

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Frozen pipes are essentially a non-issue for homes in Hawaii, since sea-level and most residential areas never get cold enough to freeze water lines. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Royal Kunia, HI

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. Plenty of copper mains have lasted 60 years. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pack sand around it so rocks do not touch the pipe. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.
