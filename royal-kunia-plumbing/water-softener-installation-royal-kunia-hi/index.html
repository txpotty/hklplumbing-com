    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Royal Kunia, HI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HKL Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.
