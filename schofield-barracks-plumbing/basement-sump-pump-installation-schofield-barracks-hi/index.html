    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Schofield Barracks

    A complete sump pump system requires a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, making sure it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not re-enter your Schofield Barracks property.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Schofield Barracks

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.
