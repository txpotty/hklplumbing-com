    What Goes Into a Sewer Line Replacement in Schofield Barracks, HI

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Schofield Barracks

    Cast iron rots from the inside out. Sewer gas and moisture eat away the bottom of the pipe first, so the camera often shows a channel worn through along the invert even when the top still looks solid. Rough scale catches everything. We swap the outdoor section for plastic pipe. For cast iron under a slab, we talk through lining versus rerouting.

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Schofield Barracks

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Clay joints let roots in every few feet, cast iron scales and rusts through along the bottom, and Orangeburg tar paper pipe slowly flattens into an oval. PVC and HDPE do none of that. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Schofield Barracks

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. Backfill goes in compacted lifts so the trench line does not sink into a ditch a year later.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Schofield Barracks, HI

    Two holes instead of one long trench. Pipe bursting drags a cone-shaped head through the old line, cracking it outward while new fused HDPE follows right behind. The other option is a cured-in-place liner. Lining puts a resin tube inside the old pipe and cures it hard, but only if the host pipe is still mostly round and has no major sags.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Replacement in Schofield Barracks, HI

    Orangeburg is tar-soaked wood fiber. Many mid-century houses got it, and by now most of it has blistered and flattened enough to snag waste. Clay holds up better, except at the joints. Several bad spots on one run usually make full replacement the better buy.

    Commercial Sewer Lateral Work in Schofield Barracks, HI

    Apartment buildings, offices and restaurants in Schofield Barracks, HI need lateral work too. Bigger flow and heavier grease mean careful sizing, extra cleanouts for jetting and attention to the grease interceptor tie-in. Some jobs need traffic control. We schedule around tenants and posted hours.
