    Basement Wall Sealing in Schofield Barracks, HI

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Warm temperatures all year mean water heaters work less hard, but constant moisture under sinks and in walls can lead to mold and rot if leaks go unnoticed. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. Those weep holes empty into the interior drain if the house has one.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Schofield Barracks, HI
