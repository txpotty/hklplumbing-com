    Common signs of a broken water line include unusually high water bills, a sudden drop in water pressure, wet spots in your yard or on pavement that don't dry, discolored water, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use.     Around Schofield Barracks, sediment and fine volcanic grit can still build up in aerators, valves, and water heater tanks, so periodic flushing is worthwhile.     Trenchless water line repair is a method of repairing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging.     Most spot repairs are finished in two to four hours. A full replacement typically takes one day, and trenchless jobs are often done in a day as well. Water is typically back on the same day.
