    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Urban Honolulu

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We cut the slab, trench the drain lines to the basin, set it, run a two-inch vent up through the house to the roof and pipe the discharge to the building drain with a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Urban Honolulu, HI

    Upgrading an old ejector can often keep the existing pit. With a sound basin, a new pump, fresh check valve, shutoff and alarm give you most of a new system for far less. We tell you if the basin is done.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Urban Honolulu

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. Grinders run pricier. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Urban Honolulu

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Some valleys and lowland areas have clay-rich soil that swells with heavy rain and can stress slabs, foundations, and buried pipes. The basin sits on gravel with backfill around it so rising groundwater cannot float it, and new concrete seals the floor and locks it down. A floating basin breaks pipe.
