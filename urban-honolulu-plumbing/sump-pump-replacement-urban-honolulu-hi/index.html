    Sump Basin and Pit Replacement in Urban Honolulu, HI

    Old clay tile or 18-inch plastic basins crack and fill with silt, and a pump in a basin that small cycles every few minutes during a heavy rain. The fix is to cut the slab, drop in a bigger poly basin with a bolted lid, and patch the concrete. A bigger basin means longer pump cycles. Fewer starts mean the motor lasts longer.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Urban Honolulu

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. We test that first. Sometimes a new switch is all you need, and sometimes the motor windings are shot. We tell you which before we sell you anything.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Urban Honolulu, HI

    Zoeller cast iron pumps, the M53 and M63 in particular, are what we pull off the truck most often because they take heat and hard cycling better than plastic housings. An existing Zoeller can usually be swapped for a current model with the same 1.5-inch outlet and float style. Need more lift? The M98 steps up to half horsepower.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Urban Honolulu

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Urban Honolulu, HI

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. Worn units get replaced, and we reset the float rod stops so the cycle is right. If your pit is wide enough, a submersible may serve you better.

    Elevator and Garage Pit Pumps in Urban Honolulu

    Elevator pits and parking garage sumps often require oil-sensing controls that stop the pump from sending hydraulic fluid or motor oil into the storm drain. Pumps and oil-sensing panels get replaced to code. Two alternating pumps on one controller mean one can fail without flooding the pit. Insurers sometimes ask for proof.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Urban Honolulu, HI

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. Narrow pits, under about 12 inches across, are where they make the most sense.

Both pumps and the alternator get swapped together. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.
