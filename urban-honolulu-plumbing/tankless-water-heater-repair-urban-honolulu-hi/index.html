    Condensing Tankless Repair in Urban Honolulu, HI

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    No Hot Water Tankless Repair in Urban Honolulu

    No hot water at all usually points to ignition, gas supply or a heat exchanger choked with scale.     Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Urban Honolulu

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup.
