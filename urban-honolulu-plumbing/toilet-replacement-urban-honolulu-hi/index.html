    High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Urban Honolulu, HI

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for 800 or higher. Higher numbers mean fewer double flushes and fewer plunger trips, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Urban Honolulu

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. We check it every time. A split ring gets a stainless repair ring, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Urban Honolulu, HI

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit.     Cracked Toilet Replacement in Urban Honolulu

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. You should replace it.     One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Urban Honolulu, HI

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more.     What a Standard Swap Includes in Urban Honolulu

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected.
