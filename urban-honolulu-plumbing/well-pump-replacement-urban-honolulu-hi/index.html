    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Urban Honolulu

    The cost of well pump replacement in Urban Honolulu, HI can vary widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $5,000 or more. This price depends on factors like the type of pump, submersible or jet, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or control boxes. We provide a detailed, upfront estimate after diagnosing your situation.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most common reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is highly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, avoiding costly mistakes.

    New well pumps usually come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. HKL Plumbing also backs our installation work with our own labor warranty. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen pump during the quoting process.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, usually less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has specific applications and benefits.

    Shallow well pumps, or jet pumps, are designed for wells where the water level is typically 25 feet or less from the pump, using suction to draw water. Deep well pumps, or submersible pumps, are placed directly in the water near the bottom of the well and push water up, suited for depths over 25 feet. Choosing the correct type is critical for efficiency and performance.

    Permit requirements vary by county. We follow local requirements for all well work.

    Regular maintenance, such as checking your well's water level, keeping the pressure tank air charge correct and fixing any leaks promptly, can extend your pump's life. Avoiding excessive water use and installing a good quality sediment filter can also reduce wear and tear on the pump.
