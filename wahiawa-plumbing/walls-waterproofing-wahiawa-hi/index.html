    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Wahiawa, HI

    Basement Wall Sealing in Wahiawa, HI

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Homes near the ocean often see corrosion on copper lines and hose bibs, and stainless or plastic fittings tend to hold up better there. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Wahiawa

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Homes are commonly built on slabs or on post-and-pier foundations, and raised homes leave much of the plumbing exposed and easier to inspect. Backfill goes in last.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Wahiawa, HI

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and tie it into a sump pit so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. It just stops puddling on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and works where a driveway or deck blocks the wall.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Wahiawa, HI

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Wahiawa

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and usually add a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60 percent. Homes near the ocean often see green corrosion on copper lines and hose bibs, and stainless or plastic fittings tend to hold up better there. The joists stop rotting. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Wahiawa, HI

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Wahiawa, HI

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer with a line to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Wahiawa

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, overlapped a foot at every seam and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and fasten it with termination bar. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.
