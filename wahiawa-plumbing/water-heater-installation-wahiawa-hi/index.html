    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Wahiawa

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Wahiawa, HI

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Some qualify for tax credits. We help you decide if one fits.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Wahiawa

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. Each gas swap gets a new connector, a drip leg, a draft test at the hood and a leak check with a detector at every fitting. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Wahiawa, HI

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Wahiawa

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it.     Tankless Water Heater Installation in Wahiawa, HI

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. Upfront cost is higher. We confirm your meter and line can handle it first.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Wahiawa, HI

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.
