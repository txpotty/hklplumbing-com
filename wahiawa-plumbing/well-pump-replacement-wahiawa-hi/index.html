    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Wahiawa

    Well pump replacement typically costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    Shallow well pumps, or jet pumps, are designed for wells where the water level is usually 25 feet or less from the pump, using suction to draw water. Deep well pumps, or submersible pumps, are placed directly in the water near the bottom of the well and push water up, suited for depths over 25 feet. Choosing the correct type is key for efficiency and performance.

    In many areas, permits may be required for significant plumbing work like well pump replacement, especially if it involves changing the well system's electrical or structural components. HKL Plumbing will help you navigate any permitting in Wahiawa, HI so your installation is fully compliant with local regulations.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, typically less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has its own applications and benefits.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also saves on labor costs to do both jobs at once.

    Low pressure, sputtering faucets, no water and high electric bills can point to pump trouble. Frequent cycling is another warning sign.

    Permit requirements vary by county. We follow local requirements for all well work.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or surprisingly high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement may be necessary.
