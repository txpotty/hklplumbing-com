    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Each maker uses its own cartridges and trim threads, so a same-brand trim kit usually bolts on without plumbing changes. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Waianae

    Repeated repairs add up.     Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Waianae, HI

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Waianae, HI

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Waianae

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. Single-handle drips mean worn cartridge seals, and multi-handle drips mean worn washers and seats. New internals end the drip. Cracked bodies get a new valve.
