    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Waianae

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly.     Backflow Testing and Certification in Waianae

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. We tag every passing device. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.
