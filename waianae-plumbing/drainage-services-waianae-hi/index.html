    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Waianae

    Drain clearing in Waianae usually runs $125 to $500 depending on the line, and hydro jetting runs $350 to $800. French drains cost more. French drain and downspout jobs usually land between $2,000 and $8,000, based on length and hardscape.

    We install and service Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty pumps. Cast iron housings hold up best. Battery backups with their own float and an alarm cover power outages and pump failures.

    Slow sinks and gurgling drains are the early signs. Sewer odor near a drain or cleanout often follows. Outside, look for water that stands more than a day after rain, soggy spots along the foundation, or mulch and soil washed onto the walk, all of which mean water is not leaving the yard the way it should.

    Most sink and toilet clogs clear in under an hour. Main lines take longer. A main line with camera work usually takes about two hours, more if the cleanout is hard to reach.

    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. Pipe repairs often require one. Replacing a section of sewer, adding a cleanout, or connecting a new drain to the sewer generally requires a permit and an inspection, and we handle that paperwork and schedule the inspector as part of the job.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It is quick and cheap. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the whole pipe wall clean of grease, scale and roots, so it lasts longer but costs more and needs a pipe that is in good enough shape to take the pressure.

    Watch for water pooling near the house after storms. Washed-out mulch tells you too. Inside, a damp crawlspace, a musty basement or efflorescence on the foundation wall all mean water is reaching the house instead of being carried away, and the problem usually starts at a downspout or a low spot.

    Our cable machines come from Spartan Tool and RIDGID, and our cameras are RIDGID SeeSnake units with built-in locators. Jetters are trailer or cart units. Good equipment matters because a camera with a locator tells us exactly where and how deep a problem is, which keeps a sewer repair to one small excavation instead of a long trench.
