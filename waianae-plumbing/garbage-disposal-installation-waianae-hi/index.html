    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Waianae

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp.     Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Waianae

    For a common 1/2 or 3/4 HP disposal, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. Cramped cabinets take some planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Waianae, HI

    An air button on the sink deck or counter turns the disposal on. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Commercial Disposal Setup in Waianae

    Restaurants and commercial kitchens in Waianae need disposals built to run all day. They usually need their own circuit, a bigger drain and a panel that runs water on its own, and some sewer districts restrict them, so we confirm the rules first. Every hour down hurts.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Waianae, HI

    The old unit usually drops free with a turn of the mount ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected.
