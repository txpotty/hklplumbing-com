    Gas Water Heater Installation Services in Waianae, HI

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Waianae

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Most run without electricity. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Waianae, HI

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Traditional Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Waianae

    Tank gas heaters are still the workhorse in most garages and utility rooms, usually 40 or 50 gallons. We connect a new gas flex line with a sediment trap, set the vent connector with a quarter inch per foot of rise and pipe the relief valve to an approved spot. Strapping where required. Sizing follows household demand.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Waianae

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Replacement in Waianae, HI

    A gas heater that quits on a weeknight still needs replacing that night or the next morning. HKL Plumbing stocks popular gas models for emergencies in Waianae, HI. Every emergency swap still gets a leak test, a draft check and a look at the water lines. Most finish that day.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Waianae, HI

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. The higher input means a bigger gas line and a dedicated vent, often PVC on condensing models. They never run out. Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is generally soft to moderate, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. We recommend pre-filtration based on your water.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Waianae

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Propane runs at a different pressure and needs a smaller burner orifice, so we install propane-specific models or convert units with the manufacturer kit where the manual allows it. We work with your supplier. We test all fittings.
