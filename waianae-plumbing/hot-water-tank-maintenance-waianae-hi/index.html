    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in Waianae, HI

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. On gas units we inspect the burner, thermocouple and vent connector. Electric units get a meter test on both elements and a look at the wiring at each thermostat, where loose terminals can scorch and melt the insulation. Both get a flush.

    Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Waianae, HI

    The anode rod is a sacrificial metal rod that corrodes so the steel tank does not. Some rural homes in Hawaii rely on rainwater catchment, which needs good filtration and disinfection to keep tanks and fixtures in safe shape. We break the hex head loose, pull the rod and check how much bare wire shows. Six inches of bare wire means replace it.

    Gas Venting Tune-Up in Waianae, HI

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. We check draft at the hood. Lint on the flame arrestor of an FVIR tank can starve the burner and kill the pilot. We vacuum it clean.

    Electric Thermostat Service in Waianae

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. When the reset trips, something caused it. We trace the cause, often a shorted element or a stuck thermostat, before resetting it. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Waianae

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. The harder the water, the sooner it matters. We drain the tank completely, refill it partway, drain it again and repeat until the hose runs clear, which on a heavily scaled tank can take four or five cycles. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Waianae, HI

    Garage and attic tanks live through bigger temperature swings than closet units, which wears out plastic drain valves and wiring insulation faster. Attic tanks need a working pan. We confirm the pan drain line is open and runs to a safe spot. Gas tanks in garages need their ignition source at least 18 inches off the floor unless they are FVIR models.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Waianae

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. That is what makes the popping noise. When a plain flush will not move them, we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems, let it sit and then flush it all out. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.

    Rental and Commercial Tank Logs in Waianae

    Landlords and property managers with a dozen units need to know which tank is next to fail. We log every tank we service. The log shows the serial date, anode condition, the last relief valve test and a rough estimate of years left, so you can plan replacements during a vacancy instead of on a Saturday night. We service 30 to 100 gallon tanks in rentals.
