    PEX Pipe Replacement in Waianae

    PEX bends around corners and comes in long rolls, so a typical repipe needs fewer fittings than copper would on the same layout.     Whole-Home Re-Piping in Waianae

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days.     Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Waianae

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Breaking a slab is messy and costly. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, which leaves the floors alone and prevents the same line from failing again. Fast too.
