    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Waianae, HI

    Warm temperatures all year mean water heaters work less hard, but moisture under sinks and in walls can lead to mold and rot if leaks go unnoticed. Insulation gets checked too. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Waianae

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. Every unit gets its own notes. We note heaters, fixtures and sewers by unit, and commercial jobs can include backflow and grease lines, giving owners a budget for repairs.

    Water Heater Inspections in Waianae

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. The serial number tells us the age, and we check for rust, look at the vent and test the relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Waianae, HI

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Older homes are where we find the most issues, usually at appliance connectors that have been there since the house was built.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Waianae

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.
