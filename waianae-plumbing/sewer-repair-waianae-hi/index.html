    Root Intrusion Removal in Waianae, HI

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Waianae neighborhoods.     Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Waianae

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Waianae, HI

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Waianae.

    Common causes include tree root intrusion, especially from older trees in established neighborhoods.     Common signs include recurring drain clogs, multiple fixtures backing up, foul odors coming from drains or the yard, gurgling noises from toilets, unusually lush patches of grass in your yard or dips in your lawn.
