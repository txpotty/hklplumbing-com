    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Waianae

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Waianae, HI

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral often runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter.     Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Waianae

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Waianae that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Waianae, HI

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.
