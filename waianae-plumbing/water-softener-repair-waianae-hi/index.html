    Resin Testing and Replacement in Waianae

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Waianae

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. HKL Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. The softener works like new.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Waianae

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. We test each part. After the repair, the valve is programmed for your household size and hardness so you have soft water by the next cycle.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Waianae

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Hard Water Symptoms and Diagnostics in Waianae

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Waianae

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Waianae, HI

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.
