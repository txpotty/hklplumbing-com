    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Waianae, HI

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. A pressure switch with pitted contacts or a plugged sensing tube can stop the pump even though nothing is wrong down the well. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. You get to see the old part.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Waianae, HI

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. Lost prime is the classic complaint. Most of the time a bad foot valve or a suction leak is letting water drain back, and we check those before condemning the pump.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Waianae

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Sand in the impellers and lightning surges are the usual culprits. We megger the motor before we pull it. That tells us whether the problem is down the hole or up top.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Waianae, HI

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. Air sneaks in through a pinhole in the suction line, the pump loses prime and the seal burns up running dry. We pressure test the line. A well that is too deep for suction needs a submersible, and we will quote that instead.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Waianae

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Replacement control boxes are easy to source. We match the motor and the wire gauge to the depth of your well.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Waianae

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. On the way up, we look at the torque arrestor and the splices. Figure on one day.

    Jet Pump Seal and Motor Replacement in Waianae, HI

    Single-stage and two-stage jet pumps wear out at the seal and impeller, and a leaking shaft seal shows up as a puddle under the motor. New pump, primed and tested. Then we watch the gauge through a full cycle.
