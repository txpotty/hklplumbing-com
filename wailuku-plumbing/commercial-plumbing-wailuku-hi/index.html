    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Wailuku

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Some valleys and lowland areas have expansive clay that swells with heavy rain and can stress slabs, foundations, and buried pipes. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.

    Commercial Fixture and Faucet Repair in Wailuku, HI

    Commercial faucets and toilets are built heavy, but they still wear. Cartridges leak. Sensor and metering faucets, wall-hung toilets, mop sinks and compartment sinks are routine for us, with Sloan, Chicago Faucets, Zurn and T and S Brass parts on hand. We can also swap in lower-flow aerators that still rinse hands well. A typical fixture repair takes an hour or two.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Wailuku

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. Passing devices get tagged. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Grease Trap Installation in Wailuku

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in Wailuku

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. Our testers handle RPZ, double check and PVB devices with annually calibrated gauges and submit results the way your provider wants them. Failed devices get rebuilt on the spot when we have the kit.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Wailuku

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. The camera goes in at a cleanout, we note distances to every defect and then locate each one from the surface with a receiver. The video is yours to keep. That record makes capital planning far easier when the board asks why the sewer needs money.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Wailuku, HI

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. We install and service high-input tank heaters, tankless racks and boiler-fed indirect systems with storage tanks. Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is generally soft to moderate, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. Scale cuts output fast. We set up a flushing and descaling schedule, and we install thermostatic mixing valves so hand sinks deliver tempered water without scalding anyone.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Wailuku

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.
