    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Wailuku

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. Attic fittings fail hard.     Copper Pipe Replacement in Wailuku

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. Plan on a higher price. We review your water and any history of copper pinholes nearby before recommending it over PEX.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Wailuku, HI

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance, and most homes are completed in two to four working days with water back on each night.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Wailuku

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. With every fixture off, a spinning leak indicator on the meter means water is going somewhere, and we use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to find it without opening walls at random. Once we find it, a temporary fix buys time to schedule the repipe.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Wailuku

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. On a slab home, we route the new lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, which keeps every supply line out of the concrete. PEX handles a freeze better than copper. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.
