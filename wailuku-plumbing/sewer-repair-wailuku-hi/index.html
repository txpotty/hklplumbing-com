    What Sewer Repair Covers in Wailuku, HI

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Wailuku, HI

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Wailuku, HI

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line with a jetter first. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, so every crack and leaking joint is covered by one jointless pipe. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Wailuku

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. A cable only punches a hole. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We camera first to make sure the pipe can take the pressure.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Wailuku, HI

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast. We clear the line with a root cutting head or a jetter with a chain flail, camera it afterward to see the condition of each joint, and tell you whether sealing or replacing those joints makes more sense than cleaning every spring. Mature trees in Wailuku yards make this a common call.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Wailuku, HI

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and locate it on the surface to keep the excavation tight. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, so nothing in the yard gets disturbed. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Wailuku, HI

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. Not every line qualifies, and we will say so.

    Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Wailuku, HI

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Patching that line is wasted money. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your Hawaii property, showing what each method costs and what it does to the yard. Depth, access and what sits on top of the line decide it.
