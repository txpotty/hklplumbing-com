    Sewer Line Replacement Methods in Wailuku, HI

    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Wailuku

    An open trench gives full access to the pipe from the house to the main. A small excavator does most of it, with hand digging near other lines. The old pipe comes out, the trench bottom gets a bed of gravel or sand, and new 4-inch PVC goes in at a steady quarter inch of fall per foot, confirmed with a level on every length. Then the camera.

    Advanced Diagnostics for Sewer Issues in Wailuku, HI

    Diagnosis comes before price. The camera shows what is wrong, the locator shows where. We sometimes add a hydro-jet cleaning first, because a line full of grease can hide a crack, then camera again so we are looking at the pipe itself. Guesswork costs money.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Wailuku

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind which leaves a single joint-free pipe in the ground. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Wailuku, HI

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Every clog then means pulling a toilet. When we replace a sewer line we add a two-way cleanout near the foundation, bringing a capped riser up to grade where you can reach it, so future cleaning or camera work is quick and cheap.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Wailuku, HI

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. We dig a launch pit near the house and a receiving pit near the main. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, which puts new pipe in the same spot without digging the yard. Driveways stay put.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Wailuku, HI

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. Lining patches the inside of the old one. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, because it leaves you with brand new pipe rather than a liner inside an old one, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Wailuku, HI

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. A hairline crack is enough. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and a root cutter clears them for a season at most, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Wailuku

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, but not forever. The inside rusts and scales until the bottom of the pipe thins out and cracks. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.
