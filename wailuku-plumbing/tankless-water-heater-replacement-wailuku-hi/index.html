    Picking a Tankless for Your Wailuku, HI House

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Wailuku

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. Sometimes a heat pump tank serves the house better, and we will say so.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Wailuku

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Wailuku, HI

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Covers and wiring take a beating outdoors. Only the highest mountain elevations ever see frost, so winter plumbing prep that matters elsewhere generally does not apply here. We check the freeze protection settings and the pipe cover kit on every outdoor swap. They are usually the easiest swap.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in Wailuku, HI

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. The vent swap is part of our job. Most of the plumbing lines up already.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Wailuku, HI

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Across 20 years the gap narrows, because one tankless may outlast two tanks, but your usage decides the payback. We price both.

    Navien Venting and Install in Wailuku

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Warranty depends on it.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Wailuku, HI

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. One bad unit can come out while the others keep running. Hand sinks and dish stations have to hit the temperatures health code requires.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Wailuku, HI

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Navien A models and Rinnai units with internal pumps can recirculate through a return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. The pump runs on a timer. Usually just the morning and evening peaks.
