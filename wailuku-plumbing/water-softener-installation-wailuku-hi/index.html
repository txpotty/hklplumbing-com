    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Wailuku

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater.     Softener Loop Installation in Wailuku

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We cut it in for you. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Wailuku

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Wailuku

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. Placing it on the main means the water heater, the dishwasher and every fixture get soft water, not just the kitchen. Sizing comes first. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Wailuku, HI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HKL Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.
