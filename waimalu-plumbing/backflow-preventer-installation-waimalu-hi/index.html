    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Waimalu

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Waimalu, HI

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Waimalu, HI

    For high-hazard applications in Waimalu, HI where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. HKL Plumbing is experienced with RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is essential for effective operation.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Waimalu, HI

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Waimalu, HI, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.
