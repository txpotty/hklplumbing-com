    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Waimalu

    A sump pump lives in a pit cut below floor level and kicks on when a float rises. Most failures come from a stuck float switch, a burned-out motor, or a check valve that lets the discharge water fall back into the pit and short-cycles the pump. We test yours with a bucket. Add a battery backup if storms knock out power.

    Floor Drain and Backwater Valve Repair in Waimalu

    A floor drain that backs up during heavy use or a storm usually points to a blockage downstream or a failed backwater valve. After clearing the line, we run a camera to confirm it is open. In homes where the street sewer backs up in storms, a backwater valve stops sewage from rising through the floor drain. Dry traps smell.

    Emergency Basement Leak Repair in Waimalu, HI

    A pipe that lets go in a basement can put an inch of water on the floor in under an hour. First, close the main water shutoff. Then call us, and a plumber will come out to stop the leak, repair the pipe, and get water back on so you can start drying the space. We can also refer a water mitigation company.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Waimalu

    Seepage at the wall or the slab joint is usually surface water pooling against the foundation. Homes are commonly built on slabs or on post-and-pier foundations, and raised homes leave much of the plumbing exposed and easier to inspect. We look at gutters and grading first, since fixing them outside often stops the seepage for far less money. Cheap fixes come first.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Waimalu

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. A camera run from the sump inlet shows us where the tile is plugged. Jetting clears it. Crushed clay tile has to be dug up and replaced with perforated PVC or corrugated pipe in a sock.

    Crack Injection for Basements in Waimalu

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy adds strength. Polyurethane is better for active leaks because it foams on contact with water.

    Commercial Basement Waterproofing in Waimalu

    Commercial basements often hold stock, files or equipment rooms that cannot sit in water. Elevator pits, floor drains and sumps all get checked together because one weak link floods the floor. Duplex pumps help. A high-water alarm warns staff before the floor gets wet.

    Waterproofing for Older Hawaii Homes in Waimalu, HI

    Older basements usually went in with block walls and little more than tar on the outside. Older homes may still have galvanized steel supply pipes or cast iron drains, both of which corrode faster in a warm, salty, humid environment. Block cores fill with water and weep through the mortar joints, so we often drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores into an interior channel. Clay tile drains fail too.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Waimalu

    Most wet basement repairs fall between $500 and $5,000, and the spread comes from what is causing the water. Regrading or extending downspouts is at the low end. Interior drains cost more since the slab gets cut, and exterior work costs most because of the digging. You get the price in writing before work starts.

    Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Polyurethane handles wet cracks. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.

    Yes, labor is covered by our workmanship warranty and parts by the manufacturer. Pumps typically carry several years. Model and serial numbers stay on file with us, so a warranty claim does not require hunting for receipts.

    Most supply and drain repairs are finished in a few hours. Sump installs take most of a day. Stack and slab repairs run one to two days because surfaces get opened and patched.

    Only some repairs need excavation. Outside membranes and new footing drains require digging to the footing. Inside repairs avoid the yard, which helps when a patio or driveway sits against the wall.

    Workmanship on pipe repairs is warranted for the term on your invoice. Pumps come with the manufacturer's warranty. Seepage repairs carry terms that match the method, and if water returns at the same spot during the warranty period we come back, retest it, and fix it without charging for the labor.

    A pipe or fitting repair usually takes an hour or two. Crack injection takes about half a day. Sump pit installation takes most of a day, and an interior perimeter drain system takes two to five days depending on wall length and how much finished space has to be cleared first.

    Tightening a loose fitting or replacing a washer hose is a reasonable DIY task. Cracked drain stacks are not. Hidden slab leaks and sump wiring also belong with a pro, since a mistake can flood the basement or leave an outlet energized in a wet space, and those repairs often need a permit.
