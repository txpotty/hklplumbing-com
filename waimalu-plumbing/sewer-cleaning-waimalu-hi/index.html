    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Waimalu

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Waimalu

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Waimalu, HI

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. When a line keeps backing up, we put the cost of regular cleaning next to the cost of a repair so you can see which makes sense, with the numbers in writing.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Waimalu, HI

    Commercial lines take a beating. Kitchens put grease into the line daily.     Residential Sewer Cleaning in Waimalu

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Waimalu, HI

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Copper was the standard for many years, while newer homes and remodels in Hawaii increasingly use PEX, which resists corrosion from salt air. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.
