    How Pipe Bursting Works in Waimalu

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. Only two pits get dug. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Waimalu, HI

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. A typical burst takes one working day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.

    Residential Sewer Replacement in Waimalu, HI

    Most of our sewer work is single-family homes. Homes are commonly built on slabs or on post-and-pier foundations, and raised homes keep much of the plumbing exposed and easier to inspect. The usual suspects are rooted clay joints, cast iron worn through at the bottom and sections that sank as the soil settled. A new line keeps sewage from coming up through the basement floor drain or a ground floor shower.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement in Waimalu, HI

    Two holes instead of one long trench. With bursting, a steel head splits the old pipe into the soil and pulls new HDPE into the same path. Lining is the other trenchless route. A resin-soaked tube is pulled into the cleaned host pipe and cured in place, which leaves a new pipe inside the old one and works well when the original still holds its round shape.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Waimalu

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. The crew trenches the full length of the lateral, removes the failed pipe and sets new PVC on compacted bedding with steady fall. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. Backfill goes in compacted lifts so the trench line does not sink into a ditch a year later.

    What Sewer Replacement Costs in Waimalu, HI
