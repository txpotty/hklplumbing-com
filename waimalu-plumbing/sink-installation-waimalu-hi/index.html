    Drop-In Sink Installation in Waimalu, HI

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style.     Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Waimalu

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Waimalu, HI

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking.     Kitchen Sink Replacement in Waimalu

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Strainers are often fused on, the disposal has to come off, and the dishwasher hose needs a fresh high loop. Then the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Waimalu, HI

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. Since the rim sits higher, the faucet has to be taller or wall-mounted, and the drain must match whether the bowl has an overflow. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Waimalu, HI

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Waimalu

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. We tie both drains into the wall with either two separate traps or a continuous waste to one trap, depending on the spacing, and add a new pair of stops for the second faucet. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.
