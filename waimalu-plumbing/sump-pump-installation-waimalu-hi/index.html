    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Waimalu

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Waimalu

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. We install exterior basins with pumps for those spots. Lots that slope toward the house need this most. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Waimalu

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Waimalu, HI

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. When we replace a pump we also check the basin for silt, look at the check valve flapper and run water through the discharge line to find any clogs. If the old one could not keep up, we size up. Testing is done with a full pit.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Waimalu, HI

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Parts are easy to find. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Waimalu, HI

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Waimalu, HI

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. A small sump basin at the low point fixes that. Access is often tight. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Waimalu

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.
