    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Waipahu, HI

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Waipahu, HI, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Waipahu

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Waipahu, HI

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Waipahu, HI

    An auto-fill line or hose connection on a pool or spa creates a straight path for chlorinated water to reach the supply. We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or remodeled.
