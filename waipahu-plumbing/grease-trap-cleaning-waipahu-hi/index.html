    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Waipahu

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster.     Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Waipahu, HI

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. Pumping alone leaves that crust in place. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. Plan on about an extra hour.

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Waipahu

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Waipahu, HI

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. A jetter takes it off the pipe wall. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. Afterward a camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Waipahu, HI
