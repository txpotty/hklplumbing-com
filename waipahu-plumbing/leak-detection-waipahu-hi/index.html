    A ceiling stain right below a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. Our job is figuring out which.     PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Waipahu, HI

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.
