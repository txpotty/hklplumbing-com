    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Waipahu

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Waipahu can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Waipahu

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.
