    Why Polybutylene Fails in Waipahu

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Most municipal water in Hawaii comes from volcanic groundwater aquifers and is generally soft to moderate, so heavy scale is less of a problem than in many places. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Finding Polybutylene Leaks in Waipahu

    A poly leak inside a wall or ceiling can run for days before you see a stain. Watch the water meter. If the meter's leak dial moves with everything off, we track the leak down with acoustic equipment and thermal imaging instead of guessing.     A whole-house poly replacement in Waipahu is a repipe, and it usually needs a plumbing permit. We file it. Rough-in inspection happens with walls open, so the inspector can see supports, nail plates, valves and the test gauge. That record helps later with insurance and resale.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Waipahu

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. Copper is stiff, heat tolerant and long proven. It costs more. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Waipahu, HI

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Copper Replacement Option in Waipahu

    Type L copper appeals to some homeowners for its long history and rigid feel. Joints are soldered or pressed. Since copper is pricier and slower to install, we walk through cost, water conditions and your plans for the house before you decide.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Waipahu

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. For slab houses, new PEX-A runs through the attic and drops down interior walls, so none of it sits under the floor. It also tolerates freezing better. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter open, so pressure at the far shower stays strong.
