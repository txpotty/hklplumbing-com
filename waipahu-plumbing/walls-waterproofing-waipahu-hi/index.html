    Drainage System Integration in Waipahu, HI

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Waipahu

    Older homes may still have galvanized supply pipes or cast iron drains, both of which corrode faster in a warm, salty, humid environment.     Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Waipahu, HI

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first.     Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Waipahu, HI

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.
