    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Waipahu, HI

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water.     PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Waipahu

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Waipahu, HI

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Code says no more than 80 psi inside the house. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and replace the gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Waipahu, HI

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Copper and poly are better pulled than burst. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, and the yard work shrinks to two pits. Rock can stop it.

    Water Line Materials We Install in Waipahu, HI

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. All of it is listed for drinking water, meeting NSF 61.
