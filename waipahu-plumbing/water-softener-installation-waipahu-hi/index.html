    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Waipahu

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds.     Softener Loop Installation in Waipahu

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Waipahu, HI

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. Putting a carbon tank in front of the softener takes out chlorine and keeps it from wearing down the resin. You will taste it. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.
