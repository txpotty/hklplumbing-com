    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Waipahu, HI

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. It works with the tank, not apart from it. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Waipahu

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Waipahu

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. They will not rust. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, plus they are easier to move into cramped utility rooms. Same plumbing as steel.

    Emergency Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Waipahu

    A tank that bursts or a pump that runs nonstop needs attention fast. Shut off the breaker to the pump. A pump that keeps running against a failed system can overheat and burn out, so we isolate the problem and get a new tank in, often the same day. Water back tonight.
