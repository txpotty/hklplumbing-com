    What We Fix on Faucets in Waipio, HI

    Ball Faucet Repair in Waipio, HI

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Waipio, HI

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. We replace the O-rings and tighten the mounting hardware, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Waipio

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Waipio, HI

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Waipio, HI

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Waipio, HI

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. Spouts install two ways. We measure the pipe stub and bring the right style, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Waipio, HI

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure.
