    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Waipio

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. A plug left in backs up the dishwasher. We also install an air gap or high loop, so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher while the disposal is draining.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Waipio

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber.     Disposal Upgrades in Waipio, HI

    Stepping up from an old 1/3 HP unit to a quieter model is a common request.     Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Waipio, HI

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The plumbing side is usually a straight swap.
