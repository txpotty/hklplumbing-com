    Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Waipio

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. We see undersized traps fail in the same few places every time.     Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Waipio, HI

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. The inlet tee sends flow down below the grease layer. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. We replace broken tees in Schedule 40 PVC and set the drop to the depth the code or the manufacturer calls for.

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Waipio

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. It sets up like candle wax on the crown of the line. Chemical drain openers soften it for a day and then it re-forms farther downstream, sometimes past the trap entirely, where it becomes a sewer problem instead of a kitchen problem. A jetter actually takes it out of the line.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Waipio

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. When that wall spalls, grease slips through to the second compartment. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Both require a full pump-out and a dry tank.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Waipio

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Without it, a full sink dump pushes grease straight through. Inspectors look for it. The air intake gets cleared too, because the fitting needs air to work.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Waipio

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Solids packed against the baffle come out by hand, with a scoop and a bucket. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Waipio
