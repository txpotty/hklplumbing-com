    Why Polybutylene Fails in Waipio

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Some rural homes in Hawaii rely on rainwater catchment, which needs good filtration and disinfection to keep tanks and fixtures in safe shape. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Waipio, HI

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.

    Polybutylene Replacement Costs in Waipio, HI

    Replacing polybutylene with PEX in an average home often costs $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size and bathrooms. Copper systems cost more. Adding a yard service line replacement often adds $1,500 to $4,000, and compared to repairing water damage from a burst, a planned replacement is usually the better use of the money.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Waipio

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Start at the water heater. You may also see it under sinks, near the main shutoff or coming through the garage wall, and we trace runs through the attic to confirm how much of the house it serves.
