    Sump Pump Maintenance in Waipio, HI: Pits, Floats and Valves

    Float Switch Inspection in Waipio, HI

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. Our plumber watches the float rise and drop through a complete cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Emergency Sump Pump Service in Waipio

    A pump that runs nonstop, hums without moving water or will not start at all needs a plumber the same day, before the pit overflows. Our plumber arrives with spare floats, check valves and a replacement pump in the van. Most jammed impellers clear in minutes. Burned-out motors get a new pump installed before we leave, so the pit is protected again that day.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Waipio

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. Our plumber pulls the unit out so we can see the cord and the shaft seal. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Waipio

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. In elevator pits, the pump usually needs an oil-sensing control so it will not discharge hydraulic fluid. We test duplex systems and high-water alarms on a set schedule. Logs go to the property manager.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Waipio, HI

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Next, we cut power to the main pump and watch the backup clear the basin by itself. Alarms get tested too.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Waipio

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Waipio, HI

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor calls for a new pump, so we carry replacements and never leave the pit unprotected overnight.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Waipio

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. Our plumber reads the model number off the label and orders the matching float or seal kit. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne get two different checklists. We know both.
