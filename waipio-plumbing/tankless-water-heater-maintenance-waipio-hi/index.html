    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Waipio

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Waipio

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Waipio

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A heavily scaled unit burns more gas per shower than a clean one, and that adds up each month. You get the maintenance price beside the likely repair cost, and the decision is yours.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Waipio, HI

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations.     Tankless Vent Inspection in Waipio

    Gas tankless heaters push exhaust out through a vent that has to stay sealed from the heater all the way to the termination outside the house. Condensing units use PVC or polypropylene vent pipe, pitched back toward the heater so condensate drains. Every joint gets checked, and we test for carbon monoxide by the heater. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.
