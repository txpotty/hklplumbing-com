    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Waipio

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Waipio, HI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HKL Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Waipio

    Most installs cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. Adding a new softener loop usually adds $500 to $1,500, depending on how easy it is to reach the main line.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt. Conditioners alter the minerals instead, and softeners are stronger for very hard water.

    Salt-based softeners add a small amount of sodium. Plenty of homeowners add an RO system at the kitchen sink for drinking and cooking.

    Homes with hard water usually benefit from one. Some rural homes in Hawaii rely on rainwater catchment, which needs proper filtering and disinfection to keep tanks and fixtures in safe shape. A softener keeps that scale out of heaters and fixtures.

    Less scale in pipes and appliances is the big one. Appliances last longer, you use less soap and detergent, skin and hair feel better and cleaning shower glass gets a lot easier. Clothes come out softer.

    Adding salt every 4 to 8 weeks is most of the work. Once a year a plumber should check for salt bridges, worn seals and injector clogs. Do not let the salt run out.

    Some homeowners try it, but we recommend a plumber. A mistake on the bypass, drain or programming means leaks or water that never softens. It also protects the warranty.

    Sized and installed correctly, a softener should not noticeably drop your pressure. A pressure drop after install usually means the valve is too small or a filter is clogged.
